What we know

  • 2023–2025: real builds. He filmed real DIY builds himself in Brazil — handheld, rural house, his own hands.
  • From Jan 2026: AI build videos in one fixed format. One locked wide shot of a room or yard, the same-looking guy in black cap + plain tee, the build appears step by step, ends with LED lights switching on / a reveal.
  • Since Feb 2026 almost every reel is AI, and posting went from ~10/month to 2–4 a day (76 posts in Oct 2026, all reels counted).
  • AI reels: 243 of 345, 7.6B of 9.0B views, 42 of the top 50.
  • Voice-over in the AI era: fast third-person storyteller (~86 words in ~20 s) opening “Guy had a genius idea…” / “Did you know…” / “You know that…”, ends on a payoff line. Real-era reels have no speech.
  • Posting time: most posts go up 6–10 PM Brazil time (4–8 PM US ET).
  • Money: he also sells PDF build guides via Linktree.
  • Video notes: Qwen video notes are filled for 60 of 345 so far (rest pending).
