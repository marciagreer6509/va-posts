What we will do

Four steps. Each one names the right place and the right day, so nobody comes to Malout on a Tuesday.

1

Your Google Maps listing

  • Your clinic already shows on Google Maps as "Bansal Skin Clinic", but no one manages it yet. We will take charge of it for you.
  • Correct timings, photos of your clinic, your list of treatments and a WhatsApp button.
  • A separate listing for your Sunday sitting in Malout.
  • Your happy patients get a link to leave a Google review, so your rating goes up with genuine reviews.
2

A WhatsApp updates group

  • A WhatsApp group where only the clinic posts, so patients get updates without any chatter.
  • Patients join it themselves by a link. Nobody is added without asking.
  • 2–3 posts a week: a simple skin or hair care tip, your timings, the Sunday reminder for Malout.
3

Facebook & Instagram ads, only around your clinics

  • Shown only to people living near Bathinda and near Malout.
  • Bathinda ads run all week. Malout ads run from Thursday evening to Sunday morning, right before your sitting.
  • Punjabi posters for most people, English posters for people who use their phone in English.
  • One tap opens a WhatsApp chat or a phone call with your clinic.
4

Malout Live, on a fixed schedule

  • Your posts on Malout Live already reach many people. We suggest one post every two months, at each change of season, with the new Malout poster.
