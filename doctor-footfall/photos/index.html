SHOT LIST

Clinic photos, shot by shot

For the Google listing · 8 photos + 1 short video · about 15 minutes. Take 3–4 of each and keep the best.

Before every shot

Phone sideways (except 1 upright of the doctor)
Daylight + all lights ON
Wipe the lens first
No zoom — walk closer
No filter, no beauty mode, no text
No patient faces

Outside (1–3): where to stand

1across the road, opposite the shutter

25–6 steps back from the door

3about 20 steps down the road, looking back

1Shop front from across the roadCOVER

Stand: on the other side of the road, straight opposite the shutter.

Frame: the whole yellow board + the open shutter + a strip of road. Phone level, board straight.

Tip: wait for a gap in traffic. Take 3–4. If the board lights up, one more after 5:30 pm.

2Board + entrance, closerEXTERIOR

Stand: 5–6 steps back from the door.

Frame: the board big and readable, the door open, a bit of the inside showing.

Tip: don't zoom — walk closer instead.

3The street, with the clinic in itEXTERIOR

Stand: about 20 steps down the road, looking back at the clinic — from the side patients come from.

Frame: the yellow board + 2–3 shops on each side + the road.

Tip: one from each direction. This is the photo that helps people find the door.

Inside (4–6)

4Waiting area / receptionINTERIOR

Stand: in the doorway or a corner, phone at chest height.

Frame: as much of the room as fits — chairs, counter, walls.

Tip: chairs straight, nothing lying on the seats, floor clear.

5Doctor's consulting roomINTERIOR

Stand: in the corner by the door.

Frame: his desk and chair + the examination couch.

Tip: clear bags, bottles and cups off the desk first.

6Treatment room + laser machineINTERIOR

Stand: in a corner, so the couch and the machine are both in.

Frame: the whole machine, top to wheels, and the couch with a clean sheet.

Tip: tuck the cables away.

The doctor (7–8) + video

7Doctor at his deskTEAM

Stand: across the desk, phone at his eye level — not from above.

Frame: chest up, white coat, looking at the camera.

Tip: light on his face (door or window behind you). Take 6–8 and keep the best; also 1 upright.

8Doctor with a patientAT WORK

Stand: behind the patient's shoulder.

Frame: the patient's back and shoulder only, the doctor's face — explaining or examining.

Tip: ask the patient first — or let a staff or family member sit in as the patient.

9Bonus: 15–30 sec walk-in videoVIDEO

How: start across the road, walk slowly to the board, in through the door, past the waiting area, end on the doctor at his desk (he can smile or nod).

Keep: under 30 seconds, one take, no talking, no music.
