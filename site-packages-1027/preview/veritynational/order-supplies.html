Order · after you send

What happens after you send

  1. We read your order on the next working day and check the items against what you told us you are storing.
  2. We write from boxes@veritynational.com confirming the total with postage, plus a link for paying securely. Your card is not touched unless you follow it.
  3. Once you have paid, the parcel is wrapped and sent inside two working days; a tracking reference comes after it.

Changed your mind before paying? Just ignore the e-mail or reply to cancel; there is nothing to undo. After delivery, unopened packs can be sent back within thirty days — see refunds and returns.

Questions first? Write to boxes@veritynational.com. We supply goods only: please do not send originals.
