Purpose of this website

Here we list our storage goods with their sizes, materials and prices, and offer a form for asking to buy them. A sent form is an order request only; it binds neither side until we confirm the order by e-mail and you complete payment through the secure link we send. Prices are in pounds sterling. We take care that every size and material is stated correctly; if we find an error before dispatch we will tell you and you may cancel without charge.
