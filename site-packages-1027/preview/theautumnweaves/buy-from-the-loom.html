What happens after you send it

  • We read every sheet ourselves and write back within one working day to say what is on the shelf, the dispatch date and the exact total.
  • In that e-mail sits a secure link for paying; until you click through it the sheet costs you nothing, and you may alter the order or let it lapse in the meantime.
  • Paid pieces leave Galashiels within three working days on a tracked service, folded in a cotton bag.

We post across the UK and to Europe, North America, Australia and New Zealand. Questions before ordering? Write to loom@theautumnweaves.com. The full terms are on postage and delivery and returning an item.
