Purpose of the site

A catalogue of three fixed-price design packages, priced in euros, plus a form for requesting a written scope. Package descriptions are correct on the revision date, yet a description by itself is not an offer capable of acceptance.

A request is not yet an agreement

A submitted form or an e-mail counts only as a request. Agreement begins when the studio has issued a written scope and you have accepted it in writing. From then on that scope, read together with the payment and refunds page, fixes the work, the hand-over date, the price and the cancellation rules.
