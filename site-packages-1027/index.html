What these sites are — read this first

Every site is built to be 100% compliant for Google Ads: a real business in a whitehat niche, with nothing on any page that a policy reviewer could call misrepresentation. Where a domain name suggested a restricted or licence-gated trade, the business was pivoted to the nearest safe niche that still fits the name — the reason is written on each card.

  • A real business with a purpose on every site: a price list on every product or package, an order or booking form with a running total, an About page, delivery and returns/refund pages (shops) or a cancellation and refund page (studios), and the business name, city and mailbox in every footer.
  • Every site passed six checks, re-run on the final build: page quality and contrast, word floors, a real browser drive (consent, menu, links, form), the batch rulings, the restricted-word sweep (no restricted trade named anywhere, not even to deny it) and the real-business check. Across the ten: no shared 8-word phrase, font, colour, image, filename or class name.
  • No fabricated proof anywhere. No testimonials, star ratings, customer counts, client logos, awards, certifications, founding years, founder names or registration numbers.
  • Street address and phone are NOT on the sites yet. They are never invented. Send the real ones per site and each site is rebuilt in about a minute — nothing else changes.
  • The cookie-consent banner is honest. It stores one preference and nothing else; no analytics or ad tag is installed, and every privacy.html says so.
  • Every photograph is committed in the package (WebP, under 50 KB, open-licence, credited on the terms page). Most show the kind of goods, not the shop's own stock — your own product photos are the best upgrade.

Read this before uploading anything

  1. Upload to any domain — nothing to set first. Canonical and Open Graph URLs are plain paths, and the form's redirect is filled in by the page itself from whatever address it is served on. The brand's own address is still written in two places on purpose: the mailbox the form posts to, and sitemap.xml plus the Sitemap: line in robots.txt. Edit those two by hand if the domain you use is a different one.
  2. The domains are fresh. All ten were on Sav.com "coming soon" parking with no mail on 27 October — nothing to preserve.
  3. Before running ads on a site: send its street address and phone (it gets rebuilt), and confirm its prices and postage figures.
  4. Create the mailbox in cPanel, then submit that site's form once and click the activation email FormSubmit sends back. One activation per site. Until that is done, enquiries silently go nowhere.
  5. Stagger the launches and spread the hosting. These sites are deliberately unrelated to each other; putting them all live on one IP on one day undoes that.
  6. Never cross-link them. No footer link, no "our other sites", nothing.
  7. If you add analytics, edit that site's privacy.html in the same change. Every site currently states truthfully that there is no analytics and there are no cookies.
  8. The 404 page is wired for Apache (.htaccess carries ErrorDocument 404 /404.html). On other servers point the error page at /404.html yourself.
