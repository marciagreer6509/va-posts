Tea Tree and Oregano Oil: What These Two Botanicals Bring to Nail Care

Plant oils have been part of foot care for a long time, and two of them come up again and again whenever people talk about stubborn toenail fungus: tea tree and oregano. Both are essential oils, meaning concentrated extracts pressed or distilled from the plant, and both are used in small amounts rather than poured on straight from the bottle.

Tea tree oil comes from the leaves of an Australian shrub. It has a sharp, fresh smell and a long history as a natural antifungal that is also used to calm irritated skin. In a nail formula its job is to support healthy nail recovery while keeping the skin around the nail comfortable. Oregano oil is the stronger of the two. Toenail Basics: Trimming, Tools and Habits That Keep Nails Healthy

Look at your nails while you trim. A healthy toenail is smooth and evenly colored.
