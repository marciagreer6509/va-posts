Returns and refunds

Changed your mind, or met your match?

Puzzles can come back within thirty days of delivery. Here is how a return works and when the money reaches you.

The return window

You may return any puzzle within thirty days of the day it was delivered, for any reason. Under UK consumer rules you already have fourteen days to cancel an online order; our window is longer on purpose, because some puzzles take a while to decide about.

Condition

Solved or unsolved is fine. Taken apart is fine. We ask that every piece comes back, with the tray, box or bag it arrived in, and that the wood is not marked, cut or glued. An opened solution envelope does not matter. A puzzle missing pieces cannot be resold, so we can refund it only in part, and we will tell you the amount before you post it.

How to send it back

E-mail puzzles@4elogic.com with your name and the puzzle you are returning, and we reply with the workshop return address. Pack it as it came, use a tracked service and keep the receipt until the refund arrives.

Who pays return postage

If you are returning a puzzle you simply do not want, the return postage is yours to pay. If the puzzle is faulty, damaged in transit or not what you ordered, we pay: we e-mail a prepaid label, or refund your postage receipt alongside the puzzle.

Refunds: how and how fast

Money returns by the route it came, the same card or account via the same payment service, within five working days of the parcel reaching us. When you simply changed your mind, we repay the puzzle price and the original delivery charge for the cheapest region option you could have chosen. For a faulty or wrong item we refund everything, including all postage. Allow your bank its own few days to post the credit.

Faulty or damaged puzzles

A sliding block that sticks, a burr stick that has split, a box panel that will not move as the solution card says: these are faults, and they are covered whenever they show up within the first year of ownership, not only within thirty days. Send a photo and a short description; often we can post a replacement piece rather than asking for the whole puzzle back.

Exchanges

If a grade turns out too easy or too hard, we are happy to exchange: return the puzzle in the condition above and name the puzzle you would rather have. Dearer swap: we e-mail a link for the gap. Cheaper swap: the gap comes back to you once the first puzzle reaches the bench. We cover the postage on the replacement.
