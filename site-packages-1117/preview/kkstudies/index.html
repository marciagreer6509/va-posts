What goes in the parcel

Paper tools for revision that actually happens

Undated 12-week study planners

Twelve weekly spreads with no printed dates, so the first page is whatever Monday you decide to begin. Each spread has a column per day, a goals box and a short look-back line for Sunday evening.

Revision card sets with rings

Three hundred A7 cards, punched in one corner and threaded on three metal binder rings. Five pastel shades let you colour code topics without buying separate packs.

Desk organisers in recycled card

A flat-packed desk tidy folded from thick recycled board. It holds an A5 planner upright, two decks of revision cards and a row of pens and highlighters.

Pastel highlighter sets

Eight soft colours with chisel tips that draw a broad band or a fine underline. They match the card shades, so one colour can mean one topic across planner, cards and notes.

Exam-season bundles

One planner, two ring sets of cards and the eight highlighters, packed together for the run-up to exams. It costs the same as the three items bought one by one.

How we work

A small stationery shop that keeps things simple

KK Studies is a small shop in Leicester that designs and sells a short range of study stationery. We started from one plain idea: most revision plans fail in week two, not week one, because the planner has dates printed in it and a missed week makes the whole book feel spoiled. So the planners are undated, the cards come on rings so you can reorder topics, and the desk organiser has a slot sized for each of them.

An order runs the same way every time. You tick items and set quantities on the order sheet, then press send. We read it, check stock and postage for your address, then write back with a costed list showing items, postage and the sum due. Underneath sits a secure pay button; until you press it, your bank balance is exactly as it was. Once payment lands we pack the parcel, usually within one to two working days, and send it with tracking so you can see where it is.

We care about paper that takes ink without bleeding, rings that open with a thumb and close firmly, and card that is genuinely recycled rather than just brown. We keep the range small so each item is properly thought through.

What we will not take on

  • No tutoring and no lessons of any kind; we sell paper goods.
  • No exam promises. A planner helps you organise revision; the marks are yours.
  • No dated diaries, because a printed date is the thing we set out to remove.
  • No international post; parcels go to UK addresses only.

If you need a dated academic diary or a subject textbook, a general bookshop will suit you better. We would rather say so than sell you the wrong thing.

Questions people ask

Before you order

Are the planners dated?

No. Every weekly spread is blank where a date would sit, so you write in the week yourself. Start in September, after a half-term or six weeks before an exam; nothing goes to waste if you pause for a fortnight.

What size are the cards?

Each revision card is A7, which is 74 by 105 millimetres: big enough for a definition and an example, small enough to hold in one hand on the bus. One face is ruled, the reverse is plain.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are packed within one to two working days of payment. Royal Mail tracked post then takes two to three working days to most UK addresses, a little longer to the Highlands, islands and Northern Ireland.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Anything still sealed and unused may be posted back to us inside 30 days of arriving, and we refund it. The returns page sets out how to send them and when the money reaches you.

Do you post abroad?

No. We only post to addresses in the United Kingdom, including Northern Ireland and the Scottish islands. We do not ship to the Channel Islands, forces addresses or any other country.

Do you do school orders?

Yes, for stationery. A school or class can order planners, card sets or highlighters in larger numbers through the order page; mention the quantity in the message box and we reply with the postage for the full parcel before anything is paid.

Pick a planner, a card set or the full bundle, and we will reply with the total and the postage.
