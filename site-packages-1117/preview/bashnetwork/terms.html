The terms of using this site

The rules for bashnetwork.com and orders placed with Bash Party Goods. Last updated November 17, 2026.

What bashnetwork.com is for

This site belongs to Bash Party Goods, a small paper-decoration maker in Dayton, Ohio, USA. It shows our garlands, bunting, tassels, confetti and table kits, prices them in US dollars, and takes orders.

From form to sale

An order form is a wish list until two things happen: we write back confirming it, and you pay through the link in that e-mail. Only then do we have a deal. The price that counts is whatever the site listed when your form reached us. Should a shade sell out in between, you choose a different one or drop that line.

Fair use of the site

Keep spam, malicious files and anything illegal out of our form, and leave the workings of the pages alone.

Who owns what

Words, page design, drawings and icons here are Bash Party Goods’s own. Linking to any page is welcome. The photographs stay the property of the people who took them and appear under the licenses credited at the bottom.

Limits

We describe sizes and colors as carefully as we can, yet every screen renders shades a little differently. Paper burns, so hang it well clear of candles, lamps and heaters. As far as the law permits, what we owe on any order tops out at what was paid for it, and your statutory consumer rights stay fully intact.

Which law applies

Ohio law governs these terms; disagreements go before Ohio courts. In short, the State of Ohio sets the rules.

Updates

These terms may be revised. An order follows the wording posted here on the day it was placed.
