Privacy notice

How Alea Marketing Firm handles what you send through aleamarketingfirm.com. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Face one

Who we are

This notice covers the website aleamarketingfirm.com, run by Alea Marketing Firm, a newsletter studio in Tucson, Arizona, USA. Questions about it go to studio@aleamarketingfirm.com.

What the booking form collects

When you use the booking form, it collects the package choices you tick, your name, your email address, your shop's name and website if you give them, the two short answers about your list and start time, and your message. We use them for one purpose: to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to arrange the work you booked.

The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, an email forwarding service, which passes the contents on to us by email. We keep booking emails for as long as we work together and for up to two years after, then delete them.

The cookie question

On a newcomer's opening visit to the studio's pages, a small dice-green card in the corner asks how you feel about cookies. Whichever button you press, the answer is written down once, as an entry called alea_cc in your browser's local storage, purely so the card stays out of your way afterwards. No other part of the site looks at that entry.

Truthfully, there is nothing else to switch on: aleamarketingfirm.com drops no cookies itself and runs no visitor counting or ad tracking. Should the studio ever add a visit counter, it would wait for your yes on that card, and this notice would be rewritten before it went live. The two typefaces are served by Google's font service, which, like any server handing over a file, sees the IP address asking for it.

Lists, ours and yours

Nobody's inbox address or shop details leave this studio for money or favors, and writing to the studio does not sign you up to anything. The only newsletters we send are the ones shops pay us to send to their own subscribers.

What you can ask of us

Any visitor may write and request a copy of whatever the studio holds about them, a fix for anything wrong, removal of it all, a portable export of those details for some other newsletter studio, or a halt to its use. Under the GDPR, people in the European Union and United Kingdom hold those same five rights: access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. Under California law, residents may learn what was gathered, ask for its deletion, opt out of its sale (there is never a sale here) and expect identical service after asking.

Requests go to studio@aleamarketingfirm.com. Expect an answer inside thirty days; we may check that the request really comes from the address on file.

When this notice changes

A revised notice replaces this one on the same address, with the new date printed under the heading.
