Payments and cancellation

What each package costs, when it is billed, and exactly what comes back to you if you cancel at each stage.

Face one

How payment works

Each booking gets an email from studio@aleamarketingfirm.com spelling out the package and price, plus a link for paying securely. Until you click it and pay, your shop owes the studio nothing. Prices are the ones listed on this site, in US dollars: setup $350, two sends $290/month, four sends $490/month, list clean-up $180, and an extra seasonal send $95.

  • Newsletter setup ($350): a deposit of $175 when you confirm, and the remaining $175 when you approve the finished template.
  • Monthly tiers: billed at the start of each month, in advance, for that month's sends.
  • List clean-up ($180): paid in full when you confirm.
  • Extra seasonal send ($95): billed once, in the month the send goes out.
Face two

Cancelling, stage by stage

Setup

  • Change of heart before the first sketch: every cent of the $175 deposit comes back.
  • Cancel after you have seen the first template draft: the deposit covers the work done and is kept; the remaining $175 is never charged, and the draft is yours to keep.
  • Cancel after you approve the template: the setup is complete and the balance is not refundable.

Monthly tiers

  • Cancel any month by emailing us before your next billing date. There is no notice period and no exit fee.
  • If you cancel during a month you have already paid for, any send that has not yet gone out is refunded: $145 per unsent issue on the two-send tier, $122.50 per unsent issue on the four-send tier. Sends already delivered are not refunded.

List clean-up

  • Cancel before we receive access to your account: refunded in full.
  • Cancel after the bounce and consent passes but before the re-engagement send: $90 is refunded.
  • Once the re-engagement send has gone out, the clean-up is complete and not refundable.

Extra seasonal send

Cancel any time before the proof is approved and the $95 is refunded, or not charged at all if it has not been billed yet.

Face three

Revisions

Setup includes two rounds of changes on the template and one on the welcome email. Each monthly issue includes one round of edits after the proof. Further rounds are not charged when the change is small; if a request means rewriting an issue from scratch, we will say so before doing it and agree the timing with you.

If we cannot deliver

If the studio misses a scheduled send for any reason on our side, you choose: we send it at the next agreed time, or that issue is refunded in full. If we ever have to stop working with a shop altogether, we give at least one full month's notice, finish the paid month, and refund anything unsent.

Refund routes

Any refund travels home along the route it arrived by, so a card payment returns to that card, and we send it off within five business days of confirming your cancellation in writing. Ask anything about a payment by writing to studio@aleamarketingfirm.com.
