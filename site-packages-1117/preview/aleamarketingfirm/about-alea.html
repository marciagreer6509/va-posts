About Alea

A small newsletter studio in Tucson, Arizona, writing for independent shops across the United States.

Face one

What the studio is

Alea Marketing Firm is a small studio that does one job: it writes and sends email newsletters for independent shops. Bookshops, plant shops, record stores, yarn and fabric shops, frame shops, gift shops and makers with a counter of their own. We work from Tucson, Arizona, and because everything happens by email, the shops we write for can be anywhere in the United States.

The name comes from the Latin word for a die. Running a small shop can feel like a roll every week; the newsletter should not. Ours is the steady, predictable part: the same fixed price each month, issues that arrive when they are meant to, and a report that tells you what happened in plain language.

Face two

How a job runs

  1. You get in touch. Use the booking form or write to studio@aleamarketingfirm.com. Tell us about the shop and which package interests you.
  2. We reply with questions. Usually within one working day. We ask about your list, your sending account and what you would like the newsletter to do.
  3. Confirmation and payment link. We confirm the package, start date and price by email. Payment happens only when you open the secure link in that email, never earlier.
  4. The work. Setup, clean-up or the first monthly issue, each with a proof for you to approve before anything is sent.
  5. Each month after. Your notes in, an issue written, a proof, your yes, the send, and a plain report.

All of this happens by email. We do not need to visit the shop, and you never need to learn a new tool: we work in your own sending account and you keep full control of it.

Face three

Pips we play, pips we pass on

We gladly write in your voice, send only to people who asked to hear from you, show you every issue before it goes out, and tell you honestly when a month went quietly.

We will not take on work outside newsletters. That means:

  • No paid ad management.
  • No buying or renting lists.
  • No social media posting.
  • No promised sales numbers.

We also write only for independent shops, not chains or franchises, and we turn down shops whose lists were gathered without permission until a clean-up has sorted that out.

How to reach us

Email studio@aleamarketingfirm.com any weekday between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. Arizona time, or start with the booking form. Payment terms and what happens if you cancel are on the payments and cancellation page.
