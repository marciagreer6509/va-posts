Face three

How a month runs

Alea Marketing Firm is a small newsletter studio working from Tucson, Arizona, writing for independent shops wherever their customers open email. We started from a simple observation: most small shops already have a list of people who asked to hear from them, and most of those lists sit quiet because nobody has the hour a week it takes to write something worth opening.

So we take that hour off your hands. You keep running the shop; we keep your newsletter going out on a steady rhythm, in your voice, with your photos and your news. The issue should read like a note from the person behind the counter, not like a flyer.

  1. First note. You write to us through the booking form or by email. We reply with a few questions about your shop, your list and how often you would like to be in people's inboxes.
  2. Setup week. We build the template, connect a sign-up form to your website and write the welcome email. You approve each piece before it goes live.
  3. Each month. You send photos and a few lines about what is new. We turn them into an issue, send you a proof, take one round of edits and schedule it.
  4. The report. A few days after each send, a short plain-English note lands in your inbox: opens, clicks and one suggestion for next time.

What we value is simple: a subject line that tells the truth about what is inside, a list made only of people who chose to be on it, and an unsubscribe link that works the first time. The studio's no list is short and firm: it never writes for chains or franchises, never mails bought addresses, and never promises a number of sales, because no honest studio can.

Face four

Questions shops ask first

Which email platform do you use?

Whichever one your shop already pays for, as long as it can handle double opt-in, segments and scheduled sends. If you have not picked one yet, we walk you through the choice during setup and the account is opened in your shop's name, never ours, so you are never locked to us.

Do we keep our list?

Yes, always. Your subscribers belong to your shop. We work inside your account with the access you give us, we never copy the list into a tool of our own, and when you leave you simply remove our access.

How often should a shop send?

For most independent shops, twice a month is a comfortable start: often enough to be remembered, rare enough that nobody feels crowded. Shops with weekly arrivals or events usually grow into four sends. We will tell you honestly if we think fewer would serve you better.

Can we approve before sending?

Every issue. You get a proof by email with the subject line and preheader written out, you reply with changes or a simple yes, and nothing is scheduled until you have said yes.

How do we cancel?

Send one email to the studio before your next billing date. There is no notice period and no exit fee; the month you have paid for still goes out as planned unless you ask us to stop it.

Do you write the product copy?

Inside the newsletter, yes: short descriptions, a headline for each feature and the button text. We work from your notes, tags and photos. Product pages on your own website are outside what we take on.
