Two sends a month

The comfortable starting point for most independent shops. One issue early in the month leads with what is new on the shelves; the second, two weeks later, carries an event, a staff pick, a restock or a short story about the shop. Twice a month keeps you in mind without crowding anyone's inbox.

Four sends a month

For shops with a lot moving: weekly arrivals, regular in-store evenings, a busy season or a list large enough to split. At four sends we start to use segments, so that people who only ever click on one kind of item hear more about that kind of item, and newer subscribers get a slightly different lead story from long-time readers.

Face two

What happens each month

Near the start of each month we send a short email asking what is new. You reply with a few lines and some photos, as rough as you like. From that we write each issue:

  • The subject line and preheader. Written together, because they are read together. They say what is inside honestly and in your voice; we never use fake reply prefixes or invented urgency.
  • The body. A lead story, a few featured items with short descriptions and button text, and a closing note. Usually two to three hundred words: enough to read on a phone in a minute.
  • The images. Your photos cropped, color-balanced and compressed so the issue loads quickly on a phone connection.
  • The proof. A test copy goes to you. You reply with changes or a yes. One round of edits per issue is included; nothing is scheduled without your approval.
  • The send. Scheduled for the day and hour your list tends to open, which we learn from the first few months of reports.

Face three

The monthly report

A few days after the last send of the month you get a short report by email. No dashboard to log into and no wall of charts: a plain note that says how many people opened each issue, which links were clicked most, how many people joined and how many unsubscribed, and one or two suggestions for next month.

We read every reply your subscribers send to the newsletter address too, if you forward them, and pass along anything that reads like a question for the shop. Some of the best ideas for issues come from those replies.

Open and click figures are a guide, not a scoreboard. Privacy features in some inboxes count an open that never happened, so we watch clicks and replies at least as closely, and we will never dress up a quiet month as a busy one.

Face four

Seasonal sends and planning

Every quarter we send a one-page plan for the months ahead: which weeks are busy for your shop, which sends lead with what, and where an extra issue might help. It is written so you can scribble on it and send it back.

When you want one more issue than your tier covers, for a holiday weekend, an anniversary or a late-night shopping evening, you can add an extra seasonal send for $95. It is written, proofed and scheduled exactly like the others, and it is billed once, only in the month it goes out.

Changing tier is just as simple. Move from two sends to four, or back again, by emailing us before the next billing date; the new price applies from the following month.
