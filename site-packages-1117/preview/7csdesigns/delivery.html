Where we post, and what it costs

Every parcel leaves the studio in Falmouth. Postage is charged once per order, not per print, and is shown in the confirmation e-mail before you pay.

Bearing 060°Dispatch

When your print leaves the studio

Each print is made after payment. Unframed prints are dispatched within three working days of payment and framed prints within seven, as they are mounted, glazed and crated by hand. Custom colourways start their clock when you approve the proof. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England. Transit times in the table above begin on the day of dispatch and are the carrier's usual times rather than promises.

Bearing 120°Packing & tracking

How it is packed, and how you follow it

  • Rolled prints are wrapped in acid-free tissue and sent in a rigid kraft tube with end caps, strong enough to stand on.
  • Framed prints have corner protectors and a sheet of card over the acrylic, then go into a slatted wooden crate that is screwed shut.
  • Gift tubes are the same rigid tube finished with a red wax seal; the card is tucked inside, and no price list is included.

Every parcel is sent with tracking. On dispatch day the studio sends you the tracking reference, and for gifts we can send it to you rather than the recipient so the surprise holds.

Bearing 180°If it goes astray

When a parcel goes quiet

Should the tracking stand still for five working days inside the UK, or ten working days abroad, write to prints@7csdesigns.com quoting your order. A trace is opened with the carrier straight away. If the parcel is confirmed lost, or has not turned up within the carrier's tracing period, we print and send a replacement at no cost to you, or refund the order in full if you prefer.

Import duties and local taxes on parcels outside the United Kingdom are set by the destination country and are paid by the recipient on arrival. We are happy to tell you the declared value and goods description before you order. Please check the address carefully on the order form; a parcel returned to us because of a wrong address can be sent again for the cost of a second postage. Returns are explained on the returns page.
