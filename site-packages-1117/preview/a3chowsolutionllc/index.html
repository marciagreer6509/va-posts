How the shop works

A small Houston stockroom with one job

A3 Chow Solution LLC is a small online shop that sells containers for carrying and storing lunches. The name comes from the word "chow", but nothing edible ever passes through our hands. Our shelves hold steel, glass, silicone and fabric, and every item is measured and described before it goes on the site, so you can decide whether a box suits the lunch you actually pack.

An order starts on the order page. You tick what you want, set the quantities, pick your shipping region and send the form. That message lands in our inbox; within one business day a reply comes back from orders@a3chowsolutionllc.com confirming the order and carrying a payment page you open yourself. Your card stays untouched until you choose to pay on that page. Once the payment clears, the parcel is packed in Houston, usually the same or the next business day, and the tracking number follows by e-mail.

Each item is checked before it is wrapped: clips that open and close cleanly, seals seated flat, glass free of chips, zips that run end to end. Glass is packed in molded paper trays rather than loose filler, and every parcel goes out in a box that can be recycled curbside.

We care about being exact. A listing says 34 fluid ounces only if that is what the cells hold when filled to the rim, and the washing notes say which part prefers the sink. If something is not right when it arrives, one e-mail sorts it out, and the returns page explains exactly how.

We also turn work down. We do not cook, sell or deliver meals, we do not write meal plans or give diet advice, we do not cater events, and we do not print names or logos on our containers. If you need any of those things, we are the wrong shop, and we would rather say so here than after you have ordered.

Questions we get

Before you order

Are the bento boxes leak-proof?

The lid on both bento boxes presses a silicone seal against the rim of every compartment, so dressings, dips and juicy fruit stay in their own cell and the bag stays dry. Thin liquids such as broth or syrup are a different job: pour those into a sauce pot, whose screw-free snap lid is the part of our range we call leak-proof without any conditions.

Can glass go in the oven?

The borosilicate glass bases of the meal-prep set go in a conventional oven up to 450°F. The snap-lock lids never do. Let a container from the freezer sit on the counter first, because no glass enjoys going from frozen to a hot oven in one step.

Are lids dishwasher safe?

Yes, on the top rack, for every lid we sell. The silicone seals last longer if you lift them out of the bento lids and rinse them by hand, and that is what we do with our own.

How fast is shipping?

Orders are packed in Houston within 1 to 2 business days of payment. Standard delivery then takes 3 to 6 business days to the lower 48 states and 6 to 10 business days to Alaska and Hawaii. Every parcel carries tracking.

Can I return a used item?

Unused items in their original packaging come back within 30 days, no questions asked. A box that has already carried a lunch can only be returned if it is faulty, because we cannot send a used container on to the next customer.

Do you sell replacement lids?

Not as a separate line. If a lid, clip or seal on something you bought from us cracks or warps in ordinary use within 90 days, write to us with a photo and we post the part at no charge.
