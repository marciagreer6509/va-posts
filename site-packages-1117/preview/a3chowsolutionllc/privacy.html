Privacy

Privacy notice

How A3 Chow Solution LLC handles what you send through a3chowsolutionllc.com. Last updated November 17, 2026.

The details the order form carries

Only what you type in. That means a name, an e-mail, a delivery address, the boxes and bags you ticked with their quantities, your shipping region and any note. FormSubmit, the service that turns our web form into an e-mail, relays those fields to our inbox and does nothing else with them for us. From there they are used for four jobs: confirming the order, sending the payment page, putting a label on the parcel, and answering any follow-up questions.

Order mail is kept while Texas and federal record-keeping rules require it. The only outside party that sees your address is the parcel carrier, because a label cannot be printed without one.

Cookies, and the little notice

This website writes no cookies of its own. On a first visit a small card asks how you feel about measurement tools; whatever you pick is stored as a single entry, a3c_cookie_ok, in your browser's local storage, and the card stops appearing. As of today no analytics, advertising or tracking script of any kind runs on these pages, so that stored answer is not consulted by anything. Should a measuring tool be added later, it would switch on only for people who said yes.

The two typefaces come from Google's font library; to deliver them, those servers see the IP address of your browser.

What you can ask of us

Your details are never sold, rented or traded. Any customer, anywhere, may write in and request a copy of their record, a fix to anything wrong in it, its erasure, an export in a format another service can read, or that we stop a particular use of it. People in the EU and UK hold exactly these rights under the GDPR. Californians, under the CCPA, may learn which categories we gather, have them erased, refuse a sale (we make none) and expect the same prices and service after asking.

Send any such request to orders@a3chowsolutionllc.com; a reply arrives within 30 days.
