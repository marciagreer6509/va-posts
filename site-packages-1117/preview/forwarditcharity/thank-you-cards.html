The press

Why letterpress feels different

Most cards you buy are printed flat: toner or ink sits on top of the paper and the surface stays smooth. Letterpress works the other way round. A raised plate carries the ink, and the press pushes that plate into the sheet with enough pressure to leave a shallow bed. Run a fingertip across one of our cards and you can trace the lettering with your eyes closed.

Our press is hand-fed, which means each sheet is placed by hand, printed, lifted out and laid on a rack to dry. A design in two ink colours goes through twice, with the second colour lined up against the first. Small shifts between colours are part of the method; anything that drifts far enough to look like a mistake goes into the offcut pile rather than into an envelope.

We mix ink colours by eye against a swatch book and keep a note of each mix, so a reorder of a design looks the same as the first batch. The main colours are forest, lake blue, clay, charcoal and a warm ochre.

  • Method: relief printing from a raised plate
  • Feeding: one sheet at a time
  • Inks: forest, lake blue, clay, charcoal, ochre

The paper

Cotton, with a soft deckle

Cotton paper is made from cotton fibre rather than wood pulp. It is softer and more absorbent than ordinary card stock, which is exactly what letterpress needs: the fibres compress under the plate instead of cracking, and the impression stays crisp. It also takes a fountain pen or a gel pen cleanly, so your handwriting does not bleed into the pressed design.

The long front edge of each folded card is left with a gentle deckle, the slightly feathered edge paper has when it is not cut by a blade. The other three edges are trimmed square. Cards are scored before folding so the spine lies flat and does not split.

The paper is white with a faint warmth to it, not a blue-white. Against the envelope colours it reads as quiet and a little old-fashioned, which suits a thank you.

  • Fibre: cotton
  • Edge: one soft deckle, three trimmed
  • Writes with: fountain, gel or ballpoint pen

The designs

Each design can be ordered blank inside, or with one short printed line inside in the same ink as the front. The printed lines are fixed for each design, usually a plain thank you or a few words about passing it on. We cannot change the wording, because we do not do custom printing; if you want your own words, a blank card and a good pen are the answer.

Designs come and go with the seasons. When a design sells out we say so on the order form rather than substituting something else without asking.
