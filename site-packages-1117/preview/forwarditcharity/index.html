How we work

From the press bed to your mailbox

Forward It is a small card shop in Kelowna that does one thing: thank-you cards printed by letterpress. We started from a simple habit. A note of thanks lands harder when it is something you can hold, and a kindness spreads further when the card itself asks the reader to pass one along. So every card we sell is built around those two ideas.

Each design starts as a drawing, which becomes a plate. The plate is locked into a hand-fed press and inked one colour at a time; a two-colour card goes through twice. The cotton sheet is soft enough that the plate sinks in slightly, which gives the edges of the lettering that pressed, shadowed look. We trim, score and fold by hand, then match each card with its envelope.

When you send an order through the form, nothing is charged. We read it, check stock, and send you a short note naming the sum owed, with a secure link to pay it. Once you have paid, the cards are packed flat between card stiffeners in a rigid mailer and leave within two working days. You get a tracking number for every parcel.

We value an even impression, square folds and envelopes that actually close. We would rather sell out of a design than reprint it carelessly. A few jobs we always decline: custom printing, no digital cards, no parcels beyond Canada, and trade orders start at fifty cards.

Questions people ask

Before you order

What is letterpress?

It is relief printing: raised letters and images are inked and pressed into the paper, so the design leaves a shallow bite you can feel. We use a soft, thick cotton paper because it takes that impression well and holds a fountain-pen line without feathering.

Are the cards blank inside?

By default, yes. Every design can also be ordered with a short printed line inside, such as a plain thank you. Choose blank or printed on the order form; mixing both in one order is fine.

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave Kelowna within two working days of payment. Parcels then take roughly two to four working days inside British Columbia and Alberta and longer further east; the shipping page lists every region.

Can I return cards?

Unused cards in their sleeve may travel back to Kelowna inside a 30-day window for a refund to your card. If anything arrives creased or misprinted, tell us and we replace it at our cost. The returns page has the details.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Not at the moment. We post to every province and territory, but no further. If you want cards to reach someone abroad, have them sent to a Canadian address and post them on yourself.

Can I order for an office?

Yes. A team that sends thank-you notes often tends to order a few boxed sets of twelve plus extra envelopes. One person fills in the order form and we send the payment link to that address.
