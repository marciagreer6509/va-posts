What is inside

Contents of each box

The set of eight holds eight folded A2 cards across four designs, two of each, with eight envelopes. It is the box people buy for themselves: enough for a season of thank-yous without the same card going to everyone.

The set of twelve holds twelve folded cards covering all six current designs, two of each, with twelve envelopes. The box is a little deeper and makes a good present for someone who still writes letters.

Cards in both boxes are blank inside by default. You can ask for the printed inside line on the order form, and it applies to the whole box. The cards sit upright with the envelopes behind them, separated by a thin card divider so the pressed fronts do not rub.

The boxes themselves are made from heavy paperboard with a fitted lid. They are plain apart from a small pressed label on the lid, and many people keep them afterwards for letters or recipes.

  • 8-card box: four designs, two of each
  • 12-card box: six designs, two of each
  • Inside line: applies to the whole box

Envelope colours

Five colours to choose from

  • Sage
  • Clay
  • Lake blue
  • Oat
  • Charcoal

Every card we sell comes with a matching envelope, and you choose one colour per item on the order form. Boxed sets come with envelopes in a single colour across the box, or in an even mix of all five if you prefer; say which in the colour field.

Envelopes are cut to fit A2 cards with a little room to spare, with a pointed flap and a gummed seal. The darker colours, lake blue and charcoal, look best addressed in white or silver ink; the lighter three take ordinary pen well. Packs of ten in any single colour are C$8.

  • Colours: sage, clay, lake blue, oat, charcoal
  • Fit: A2 cards
  • Pack of 10: C$8
