What happens next

We read your order on the next working day and send a short e-mail naming the full sum, postage in, with a secure pay link. Your card is untouched until you use it. Cards leave Kelowna within two working days of payment, tracked, to any address in Canada.

  • Shop: Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada
  • Ships to: all of Canada, nowhere else
  • Mailbox: cards@forwarditcharity.com
