Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Here LR Higgins Consulting sets out which details lrhigginsconsulting.com gathers, the reason for each, and the choices you keep over them. It applies to anyone who reads these pages or sends the request form.

What the request form collects

The form on the request page asks for your name, email address, business name and kind, town, team size, how you would like to meet, the documents you ticked, the estimated total and your message. Those details serve three purposes and no others: answering your request, quote a fixed price and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. Submissions travel through FormSubmit, which relays each one as an email into our office mailbox and keeps no account for you.

The corner card about cookies

A small card in the corner of your first page view offers a choice. Your answer is written into browser storage under one key, lrh_cookie_ok, purely so the card stays away on later visits. At present no script on these pages ever looks at that key. We place no cookies ourselves, and there is no visit-counting or ad tool anywhere on the site. Should one ever be added, it would load solely for people whose stored answer allows it, and this page would change beforehand.

Sharing and selling

Nobody buys your details from us, and no advertiser receives them. Details you send are kept in our mailbox and in project notes for as long as a job, and its records, need them.

Your rights

From any country, a short email lets you see the records we keep on you, fix mistakes in them, have them erased, receive them as a file you can take elsewhere, or tell us to stop processing them. These match the rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection under European data rules. People in California may additionally learn which categories we gather, require erasure, refuse any sale of their details (none ever happens here), and expect identical service after asking.

To use any of these rights, email office@lrhigginsconsulting.com. We answer within thirty days.
