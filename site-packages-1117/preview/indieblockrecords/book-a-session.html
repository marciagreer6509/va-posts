What happens next

Sending this form books nothing yet and charges nothing. We read it, listen to any rough mix you link, and write back within two working days: the total, whatever we heard in the files, and a secure link for paying half up front. Work starts once the deposit and your stems are in. The balance is due when you approve the final files.
