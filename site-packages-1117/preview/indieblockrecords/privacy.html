Privacy

How Indie Block Records handles what you send through indieblockrecords.com. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Whose desk this lands on

indieblockrecords.com belongs to Indie Block Records, a per-track mixing and mastering room in Austin, Texas. Anything about your details — a question, a correction, a request to wipe them — goes to mix@indieblockrecords.com, the one mailbox the studio reads.

What the booking form picks up

When you send a booking we receive your name, your email, the artist or project name, the jobs and quantities you ticked, the running total the form worked out, and your notes about the songs. That is enough to answer you, price the mixes and masters, and run the job if you go ahead. FormSubmit, the relay that turns a website form into an email, hands the booking over to the studio mailbox, and its part ends there.

Stems and mixdowns you upload later are used for one thing: mixing and mastering your songs. Session folders stay on our drives for ninety days after the final files go out, in case a small tweak is needed, and are erased after that unless you ask us to hold them.

Cookies, consent and that card by the mix bus

This website writes zero cookies, full stop. The card that greets a first-time visitor records a single choice under the name ibr_consent in local storage on your own device, purely so the card stays gone afterwards. Today no script on the site looks at that choice, because no visitor-counting or ad tools are wired in. If that ever changes, this page changes first and the card asks again. Our pair of display and text fonts streams in from a font-hosting service, and like any server it logs the IP address asking for them.

Nothing sold, nothing traded

Your details are not sold to anyone and are never handed over for ad targeting. They pass through three places only: the form relay, the provider that hosts our mailbox, and the card processor behind the deposit link once you book.

What you can ask of us

Under the GDPR, a visitor from Europe may request a copy of the details we keep, fix an error in them, have them erased, take them away as a portable file, or object to a use of them. Under the CCPA, a California resident may learn which details we keep, request their erasure, refuse their sale — a moot point, since the studio sells none — and expect identical service after exercising any of this. We extend the same treatment to every artist, wherever they record. Send the request to the studio mailbox and expect an answer inside thirty days.
