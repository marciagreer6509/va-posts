Terms of use

The terms for using indieblockrecords.com, the website of Indie Block Records. Last updated November 17, 2026.

The point of this website

indieblockrecords.com exists to explain how Indie Block Records mixes and masters songs, to show the per-track rates, and to take bookings. The rules on this page cover browsing the website. The actual mixing or mastering job is set out in the email we send in reply to a booking.

Sending the form is asking, not agreeing

A booking form tells us what you would like quoted — neither you nor the studio is committed by it. The job becomes real when you say yes to our emailed figure and settle the half-up-front deposit via the link we send. Stages, deposits and what a cancellation costs are on the revisions and refunds page.

Fair play on the site

Keep the booking form for genuine bookings: nothing unlawful, no floods of automated messages, no attempts to break the pages, and no links to audio you have no permission to pass on. Bookings that look like spam simply go unanswered.

Ownership

The words, layout and drawn graphics here are Indie Block Records's own; the photographs are borrowed under the licenses itemized at the foot of this page. Your songs and recordings, and every mix and master we make of them, are yours — working on them hands the studio no share and no claim.

How far our responsibility reaches

We keep the rates and process notes on these pages current, yet a website can carry a slip, and it is provided without promises beyond what the law insists on. So far as Texas law permits, Indie Block Records accepts no responsibility for indirect or knock-on losses tied to browsing it. Any responsibility the law forbids a business to exclude stays fully in place.

Which law applies

The State of Texas supplies the law for these terms, and any disagreement over them is heard by a court sitting in Travis County, Texas.

Edits to these terms

The studio may revise this page from time to time; the "last updated" line beneath the title tells you which edition you are reading.
