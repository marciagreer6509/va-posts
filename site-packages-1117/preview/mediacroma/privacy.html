Privacy at Mediacroma

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Skin tones, night exteriors, a client's product colour: the suite spends its days guarding things that belong to someone else, and your personal details get that same guarded treatment. This notice sets out what mediacroma.com gathers, the reason for each item, and how to have it corrected or erased. Mediacroma, working from Braga, Portugal, decides how these details are used. Questions go to grade@mediacroma.com.

What a booking brings us

Sending the booking page hands us the name you typed, the address to reply to, the project lines you filled in — kind of film, running time, camera, deliverables, a target date, the services ticked and the running total — and your note. Those details exist to answer you: to price the grade, to schedule the suite and, once you commit, to deliver the masters. They are never turned into a mailing list.

The booking travels to our inbox through FormSubmit, a relay that passes form entries on by e-mail and uses them for nothing else. Bookings that never become a grade are cleared out within twelve months; correspondence for a finished project is held only for the period Portuguese bookkeeping rules demand.

The one preference this site remembers

No cookie is written by mediacroma.com. On a first visit a corner panel asks whether you accept optional measurement; whatever you answer is stored as a single local-storage entry named mcr_consent, and the panel stays away afterwards. Today no script reads that entry, because no measurement or advertising tool is installed — the switch labelled analytics and performance is wired to nothing yet. Should that ever change, this page changes first, and the switch remains off unless you move it. Fonts are fetched from Google Fonts; that is the only third party your browser contacts here.

Where your details do not go

Your details are not sold, traded or lent. Beyond the form relay, the only other party that ever sees them is the payment provider behind the deposit link, and only once you have booked.

What you can ask of us

European data law (the GDPR) lets you see what we hold on you, fix mistakes in it, have it erased, take a copy in a portable form, and object to or limit any use of it. Residents of California get comparable footing from the CCPA — disclosure of the kinds of detail collected, wiping on request, a veto over selling (moot here, since Mediacroma sells nobody's details), and an even-handed grade and price for anyone who invokes them. Write to grade@mediacroma.com and expect an answer inside one month. Complaints may also be taken to Portugal's data-protection regulator.

Footage held for a grade

Camera originals and edit lists sent for a job stay on studio storage, are opened only for that job, and are erased or returned as you instruct after the masters are signed off.
