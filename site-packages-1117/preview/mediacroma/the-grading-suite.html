How a job runs

What the studio is

The studio works from Braga, in the north of Portugal, and takes work from productions anywhere. Footage reaches us by transfer link or on a drive sent by courier; review files and masters go back the same way. Nothing about the work needs you to be in the room, which is why we run review sessions over a live stream rather than in person.

The room is set up for one thing: judging colour. Controlled light, neutral grey walls, a calibrated Rec.709 reference display and the scopes beside it. It is a quiet place to look at pictures carefully.

What it will and will not do

We grade, we develop looks before a shoot, we conform edits and we master SDR and HDR deliverables. We work on short films, music videos and brand films.

  • No filming or crew
  • No editing from raw footage
  • No sound mixing
  • No ad placement or media buying

We also turn down cuts that are still changing. Grading an unlocked edit means grading shots that will be cut, then paying again for the ones that replaced them.

How a job runs

  • BookingYou send the booking form with running time, formats and references. We reply by e-mail with a written quote and a schedule.
  • DepositIf you accept, we send a secure payment link for the deposit. No money moves before that.
  • Conform and checkThe edit is rebuilt against the camera originals and compared with your reference export.
  • First passThe grade, delivered to you as a review file.
  • NotesTwo rounds included, by e-mail with timecode or in a remote session.
  • MastersRendered to your delivery sheet, checked, sent by transfer link. The balance is invoiced at this point.

The terms of each stage, and what is refunded if a project stops, are on the payment and cancellation page.

Contact

How to reach the studio

The mailbox is the way in: grade@mediacroma.com. We read it Monday to Friday, 10:00–19:00 Portuguese time, and reply with a quote rather than a sales call. For a firm price, use the booking page, which adds up the work as you choose it.
