Payment and cancellation

How Mediacroma charges for a grade, what each stage costs, and what is refunded if a project stops part of the way through.

The deposit

When you accept our written quote, we e-mail you a secure link to pay half the quoted total up front. Work is scheduled once the deposit arrives. Nothing is taken from you before you open that link and pay; the booking form on this site is an enquiry and costs nothing.

The remaining half is invoiced when the masters are ready, and the masters are sent when it is paid. Prices are those on our price list in euros: €90 per finished minute for a grade, €320 for a music video grade up to four minutes, €250 for look development, €150 for a conform, €120 for the HDR add-on and €60 for each hour of remote review.

What each stage costs

  • Booked, not startedDeposit paid, nothing yet done.
  • ConformThe €150 conform fee is earned once the conform is completed and the check list sent to you.
  • First pass deliveredHalf of the grading fee is earned when the first-pass review file reaches you.
  • Masters deliveredThe whole quoted total is due.

If you cancel

Call it off before the conform begins and every euro of the deposit comes back. Call it off after the conform is complete and we keep the conform fee and refund the rest of the deposit. Cancel after the first pass has been delivered and we keep the deposit; you owe nothing further, and the review file is yours to keep. Look development cancelled after the camera tests are graded is charged in full; before that, it is refunded in full. A remote review session cancelled at least one working day ahead is moved or refunded; a session missed without notice is charged.

To cancel, write to grade@mediacroma.com from the address you booked with. The date of your message decides which stage applies.

Revision limits

Every grade includes two rounds of notes on the first pass. A round is one list of notes, sent together, answered with a new review file. Further rounds are booked as remote review sessions at €60 per hour. Changes to the edit after the conform are not revisions: a re-cut means a new conform, quoted before we do it.

When the suite lets you down

Should we be unable to finish the work we agreed — through illness, a failure in the suite or anything else on our side — an e-mail reaches you before the end of that working day proposing a fresh delivery date, and if that date does not work for you we refund every euro paid for the stages not delivered. Files you sent are returned or deleted on your instruction.

How a refund reaches you

Money owed to you returns by whichever card or transfer paid it, inside fourteen days of the cancellation or of our notice. We do not issue credit notes in place of a refund unless you ask for one.

Questions about a payment go to the mailbox above. These terms sit beside our site terms, which are governed by the law of Portugal.
