Book a grade

Choose the work, give us the running time and tell us where the film is going. The total adds up as you choose; we reply by e-mail with a written quote and a schedule that match it.

What happens after you send this. We read your booking and reply ourselves, usually within one working day. If the quote suits you, we send a secure payment link for the deposit of half the total. Until you click it, the grade has cost you nothing, and changing your mind at the quote stage is free. The remaining half is invoiced when your masters are ready.

The studio works from Braga, Portugal and takes projects from anywhere footage can be sent by transfer link. Questions first? Write to grade@mediacroma.com.
