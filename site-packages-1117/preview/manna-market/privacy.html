Privacy

Your details, weighed as carefully as the flour

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Manna Market runs manna-market.com from Bendigo, Victoria, to sell bannetons, couches, lames, scrapers and linen bags. Here is everything that site does with information about you, set out the way we would set out a recipe: ingredients first, then method.

Ingredients: what arrives when you order

Pressing send on the order form passes us the lines you filled in: name, e-mail, street or PO box, suburb, postcode, state, the tools and how many, the tally, and your note if you left one. Those lines let us check the shelves, quote you, pack the box and send tracking. They are put to no other use.

A forwarding service called FormSubmit turns each submitted list into a plain e-mail that lands with us in Bendigo. Card numbers never touch manna-market.com; the card link we send later belongs to a separate payment company with terms of its own.

Method: browser storage, and the lack of cookies

Not one cookie is placed by this shop's pages. Answering the small consent box writes a single item, mnm_cookie_pref, into your browser's local storage, saying yes or no to analytics and performance counting. At present that item just sits there unread, since the shop runs no visitor counting and no advertising code at all. Should counting ever be added, it would only run for those who ticked yes, and this page would change first. Wipe your browser's site data and the item disappears; the box then asks again.

Left out of the recipe

Your details are never sold, swapped or lent out, and no mailing list is built from them. Order e-mails stay on file only as long as Australian tax record-keeping asks, then they are deleted.

Asking for your details

From Ballarat to Berlin, anyone can write and ask to see what we hold on them, have it fixed, have it wiped, receive a copy to take elsewhere, or stop a particular use. European visitors know these as access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection. People in California can additionally ask what we gather, ask for deletion and opt out of a sale; there has never been one, and nobody gets poorer treatment for asking. Australians are also protected by the Australian Privacy Principles.

Send any such request to bake@manna-market.com and expect an answer within 30 days.
