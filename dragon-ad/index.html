5 · The link + tracking (so you know WHICH ad sold)

  • Website URL = your DragonFlame tracking link from Blitz. Paste it exactly as Blitz gives it.
  • URL parameters box (under "Tracking" at the bottom of the ad), paste:
URL parameters (paste into Facebook's 'URL parameters' box) 
sub2=fb&sub3={{ad.name}}
  • Facebook replaces {{ad.name}} with the ad's name automatically. Name each ad clearly, e.g. V1-fire-textA, IMG2-daynight-textB. Blitz then shows sales per ad in sub3.
  • If your Blitz link already uses sub2 or sub3, tell me and I'll pick free slots.
  • Use the Facebook Preview button only. Don't click your own live link over and over: every click counts as traffic in Blitz.

The method (simple version)

  • Test the AD first, the audience second. The creative (video/picture + first line) decides 80% of results on Facebook. Run 3 ads side by side and let the numbers pick.
  • Start with 2 ad sets (same 3 ads in each):
    Ad set 1 · BROAD: United States, age 35–65+, all genders, no interests. Facebook finds the people.
    Ad set 2 · INTERESTS: United States, age 35–65+, interests: Gardening, Landscaping, Garden furniture/décor, Home and garden, Dragons, Fantasy, House of the Dragon / Game of Thrones.
  • Important limitation: we can't put a Facebook pixel on the merchant's checkout, so Facebook doesn't know who BOUGHT. It only knows who CLICKED. That's why sales are tracked in Blitz (sub3) and the kill/scale rules below run off Blitz.
  • Day 4+ (step up): build a Custom Audience of people who watched 75% of the video + people who engaged with the ad. Then build a 1% Lookalike (US) of them. Run the winning ad to that lookalike. These people already showed interest.

7 · Step-by-step launch (Ads Manager on a computer, not the phone app)

Step 0 · Before you start

  • Your DragonFlame tracking link from Blitz is ready.
  • You have: a Business Manager (business.facebook.com), a Facebook Page (neutral name + a dragon/garden profile picture), an ad account, a payment card.
  • New ad account: set time zone and currency = USD carefully. They can never be changed.
  • 🛑 Never press the blue "Boost post" button on the Page. Always use Ads Manager.

Step 1 · Campaign

  • Ads Manager → + Create → objective Traffic → Continue.
  • Name: Dragon | Traffic | US.
  • Advantage campaign budget: OFF (so each ad set gets its own fixed budget for a fair test).
  • Special ad category: none.

Step 2 · Ad set (do it once, then Duplicate for ad set 2)

  • Name: Broad 35-65 (2nd: Interests garden+dragon).
  • Conversion location: Website · Performance goal: Maximise number of link clicks.
  • Daily budget: $20. Start date: tomorrow 00:00 (gives review time, starts on a full day).
  • Audience: Location United States (People living in), Age 35–65+. If Facebook shows "Advantage+ audience", for the Broad set leave it ON; for the Interest set add the interests above.
  • 🛑 Placements → Manual: keep Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, Stories. UNTICK Audience Network and Messenger. Those sell junk clicks on Traffic campaigns.

Step 3 · Ads (3 per ad set)

  • Ad 1 V1-video-textA: a video (9:16 for Reels/Stories, 1:1 for Feed), Text A, Headline 1. Name video ads by version: V2-trailer, V3-daynight, V4-pov, V5-3reasons.
  • Ad 2 IMG1-fire-textB: Image 1 (4:5), Text B, Headline 2.
  • Ad 3 IMG2-daynight-textC: Image 2 (4:5), Text C, Headline 3.
  • Each: CTA Shop Now · Website URL = Blitz link · URL parameters = sub2=fb&sub3={{ad.name}}.
  • 🛑 Turn OFF "Advantage+ creative" enhancements (they add music, change the picture, rewrite text). Also turn off "Add site links" / "Add catalogue items".
  • Press Preview → check Feed, Reels and Stories look right → Publish. Review takes from minutes up to 24 h.

Step 4 · Days 1–3: hands OFF

  • Check once a day. Don't edit live ads: any edit restarts Facebook's learning.
  • Columns to watch (Columns → Customise): Amount spent, Impressions, CTR (link click-through rate), CPC (cost per link click), Link clicks.
  • Compare with Blitz: clicks + sales by sub3 (= ad name).
  • Reply to genuine questions in the comments. Hide spam.

Step 5 · Day 4+

  • Turn off losers (rules below). Keep the winner(s).
  • Add the other videos (V2–V5) as new ads in the WINNING ad set, or test them from day 1 instead of images (one video per ad).
  • Build the 75% video-viewers Lookalike (section 6) → new ad set with the winning ad, $20/day.

8 · Kill / scale rules (so we don't burn money)

Turn an AD off when

  • After ~1,000 impressions: link CTR under 0.8%. People aren't stopping for it.
  • After ~1,000 impressions: CPC over $1.00.
  • Spent 1.5× your payout with 0 sales in Blitz.

Scale an ad set when

  • Cost per sale (spend ÷ Blitz sales) is below your payout for 2 days in a row.
  • Raise budget +20% every 2 days at most. Big jumps restart learning and costs spike.
  • Or duplicate the winning ad set at the same $20 into a new audience (Lookalike, or the gift angle in December).

Don'ts that get accounts banned or waste money

  • No fake reviews, fake countdowns, "only 3 left", or "as seen on TV". The page says none of that.
  • Don't use the brand "Shirem" as your Page name or picture.
  • Don't make 5 ad accounts at once or switch cards often. New accounts start with a low spend limit. Let it grow.
  • If an ad is rejected: Request review once. Don't re-upload the same ad 10 times.
