5. Fix list

  1. Get Trakheesi permits for each Samana project ("Inside Dubai" type, with Samana's marketing contract attached), plus a promotion permit for the offers.
  2. Show the permit number and Madmoun QR code beside every project, and in the ads where the format allows.
  3. Show each project's DLD project number and escrow account number, with "pay only into the project escrow account".
  4. Footer: "Provident Real Estate Broker L.L.C · ORN 1933", the agent's BRN, Office 1802 Marina Plaza address, +971 4 892 5831 and an email.
  5. Use a WhatsApp number registered to a licensed broker.
  6. Add "Authorised agent – not Samana Developers' official website" near the top.
  7. Remove Avenue, Barari Heights and Sky Views (sold out). Correct prices and handover dates to Samana's site.
  8. Make the ad price match the page (750K vs 779K).
  9. Remove "High ROI", "Tax-Free Income" and "5th Largest Developer".
  10. Add under prices: "DLD 4% fee and service charges are extra".
  11. Use one company name everywhere (footer and privacy policy).

6. Not closable from outside

DLD project number + escrow per project: DLD's public projects search is behind a reCAPTCHA — one minute by hand on DLD's open-data page or the Dubai REST app.
