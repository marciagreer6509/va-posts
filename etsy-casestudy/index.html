2. The pattern (8 rules every winner follows)

  1. One hero product. An "Ultimate Annual Budget", "Budget by Paycheck" or "Small Business Bookkeeping" sheet carries the shop (20–47% of reviews). Everything else is a variation of it (dark mode, couples, biweekly, business) or a cheaper entry point.
  2. Both formats in the title. "Excel Google Sheets" in the title of most listings (PrioriDigitalStudio 80, TheSheetCode 58 of 62, TheWeeklyCrew 47 of 52).
  3. Easy beats powerful. Praise words in 1,000 reviews: easy 498, love 291, helpful 189, excel 92, organized 74, simple 69, instructions 55, quick 49, video 48, customer service 48, fun 42, tutorial 25. Complaints are almost all "too complicated / overwhelming / over-engineered" or setup trouble.
  4. Show it working. Video in the listing and a step-by-step guide: Pretty Arrow ships a video + 8 images per listing; Google Sheets delivered as a PDF with a make-a-copy link, Excel as the file.
  5. Price ladder. $1.79–2.99 entry listings in 10 of 20 shops (ExclusiveDesignLab's $1.98 budget planner has 3,467 reviews and feeds a $8–50 range); core $8–16; bundles $25–90. Pretty Arrow only reached six figures after building a high-priced bundle.
  6. Ads on the expensive items only. Pretty Arrow: Etsy Ads were only clearly profitable on $20–30 templates, not on $5 ones; she uses ads to launch new products and replaces images when a listing converts below 2%.
  7. Few, deep listings. 7–70 listings for most winners. Variations (dark mode, couples, biweekly) are separate listings to win separate searches.
  8. Narrow searches. Generic "budget tracker / planner" is crowded; the winners rank for paycheck, biweekly, couples, bookkeeping, debt snowball, sinking funds.     • 4. What the deep research found (web sources)

    • Pretty Arrow (prettyarrowbudget): moved from $2 printables to colourful Google Sheets/Excel at $5; first sale within 24 hours; 29,000+ spreadsheets sold by May 2023 (~2.5 years), ~$10k/month with a team of two; headline "$250k in under 2 years" (self-reported; the founder also sells courses).
    • Paid traffic: Facebook/Instagram ads → free lead magnet → one-time $17–27 bundle offer to email sign-ups = 2–3× return on ad spend (founder's figure).
    • SimplyOrganizedPro: $1–2.50 monthly budget sheet, ~8.6k sales in ~14 months with no social media — Etsy search alone (third-party estimate, 2024).
    • Counter-view (Better Sheets, sells off Etsy): never price a Google Sheet under $20; aim for $49+ by adding automation. On Etsy the market sits at $8–16, so: entry low, hero $15–25, bundle $35–60.
    • Not found anywhere checkable: founder stories for TheWeeklyCrew, ExclusiveDesignLab, PrioriDigitalStudio and the other big shops — our numbers above come from Etsy's own API.

    5. Animated / "3D" wedding invitations — the verdict

    Yes, with one change: sell it as ANIMATED / INTERACTIVE, with 3D as the look. Buyers search "animated", "video" and "interactive" — not "3D". EverBee: video/animated wedding invitation ≈ 370–400 searches/month (≈ 6–7k competing), animated save the date ≈ 250, "3d wedding invite" ≈ 186. Etsy: interactive wedding invitations sell at a $18 median (top quarter $30) versus $9 for flat digital invitations with 300k listings; "envelope opening" invitations have only 906 listings. Etsy policy explicitly allows "wedding invitations designed by a seller and provided as a digital download"; AI use must be disclosed and a person must be creatively involved in every item.
    It is not empty. Greenvelope (since 2008) and Paperless Post already sell animated envelope invitations with RSVP, maps and confetti — that's the bar. Our edge on Etsy: a one-time price instead of their per-event fees, a 3D-rendered look no Canva template has, and editable details. Production must be template-based (buyer fills names/date/venue) — custom per-order video work does not scale.

    6. Our launch plan from this

    1. Hero spreadsheet first: "Ultimate Budget by Paycheck" — Google Sheets + Excel, one-page dashboard, 5-minute setup video, light + dark versions. Hero price $14.99–19.99.
    2. Entry listing at $2.99 (simple monthly budget) to collect the first reviews fast.
    3. Variations as separate listings: couples, biweekly, debt snowball + sinking funds, small-business bookkeeping, wedding budget + planner.
    4. Bundle at $34.99–49.99 once 4 sheets exist; Etsy Ads only on the hero and the bundle.
    5. German add-on: Haushaltsbuch / Budgetplaner versions (765 searches/month, 93 competing).
    6. Wedding line: animated envelope-opening invitation (MP4 + link) with a 3D-rendered look + matching wedding website template ($19.99–29.99), save-the-date variant; buyer edits text in a form/Canva, we never do custom video by hand.
