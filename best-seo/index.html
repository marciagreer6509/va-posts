Best SEO: a page for every search people type next to the brand

Case study, 09 Nov 2026. Source: Search Console for all 39 of our properties, 90 days (10 Aug–08 Nov 2026), 13,990 query rows from 34 offers. This is now the BASIC standard for every offer.

THE RULE. For every country we target, take what people searched next to the brand name on all our past offers in that country. If a search type is HIGH or MEDIUM and our plan doesn't already have a page for it, it gets its own page on day 0. TV-show pages are built in every country, always.

HIGH = 1,000+ impressions, or seen on 3+ offers. MEDIUM = 150+ impressions, or seen on 2+ offers.

Why we missed pages on Mobiflexi

  1. We built from a fixed 8-page template (reviews, scam, ingredients, price, side effects, how to take, buy, pharmacy). The template wasn't checked against what people actually type in each country.
  2. We read the new offer's own data, which was nearly empty. Mobiflexi had 8 impressions when we planned it. The portfolio had 13,990 queries telling us exactly what Germans, Brits and Dutch people search next to any supplement brand.
  3. Search Console only shows searches you already rank for. No TV page means no TV query in the report, which looks like "no demand". 13 of 34 offers show TV searches. Every one of the 7 biggest offers does, at 4–24% of all their impressions.
  4. Our own safety gate blocked the show name. "Löwen" was banned outright, so a page that says honestly "Mobiflexi was NOT on Die Höhle der Löwen" couldn't even be written. The gate now bans the claim and allows the denial.
  5. "Only where searched" was applied per offer instead of per country. The right question is what people in this country search next to brand names in our niche, not what they searched for this brand.

How to run it on any offer

  1. Refresh the data: question-queries/pull.py (runs from the droplet; Google blocks this server).
  2. Run best-seo/mine.py, agg.py and matrix.py to rebuild the country × type grid.
  3. Run check_site.py <domain> --root <geo at /> to list every HIGH/MEDIUM type the site has no page for.
  4. Build the missing pages with native writers and the gates, deploy, then push 20–30 a day.
  5. Re-run every 2 weeks. New searches appear as the brand grows.

Limits: Search Console hides rare searches and only shows ones where we already appear, so real demand is higher than shown. Misspellings are counted from what the brand-strip leaves behind.
