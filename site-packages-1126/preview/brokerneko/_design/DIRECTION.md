# Neko Perch — art direction (written before any code)

**Concept, one sentence:** the page IS the wall — you read it the way a cat climbs it, hopping from one hoop-pine
ledge to the next along a dotted paw-step path, with the price of every piece burnt into the plywood edge.

**Mood:** cheerful · workshop-plain · sun-warmed.

## Type
Display Mochiy Pop One (one weight, chunky, rounded) · body M PLUS Rounded 1c (400 / 500 / 700 / 800).
Modular ratio 1.25, fluid:
- t0 0.8rem (labels, burn marks) · t1 1rem→1.125rem body · t2 1.125→1.3rem lead · t3 1.3→1.65rem h3
- t4 1.6→2.2rem h2 · t5 2→3.1rem page h1 · t6 2.2→3.3rem home h1
Body line-height 1.65, headings 1.15. Display never below 0.8rem; burn marks letter-spaced .04em.
Fallback face with size-adjust so the swap does not move the layout.

## Spacing
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 112 px. Ledge padding clamp(24px, 4vw, 56px). Gap between ledges =
one paw-path (about 110px desktop, 84px phone).

## Grid
Wall width 1180px max, 20px side gutter on phones. Ledges are 980px max and step alternately to the left and the
right edge of the wall; inside a ledge a 12-column feel (7/5 splits), 32px gutters. Body measure capped at 66ch.

## Colour roles (AA checked in the builder — it asserts every pair)
- ground `#FBF6EE` (the painted wall) · surface `#FFFDF8` (ledge top) · ply face `#F4E3C3` · ply edge `#DDB887`
- ink `#2A1D17` · muted ink `#6A5145` · theme `#8A5A44` (brackets, strapline band) · theme deep `#5E3A2A`
- accent `#3FA796` (the action colour: buttons carry INK text on it, 5.6:1) · accent deep `#1E6B5F` (text links)
- line `#E4D5BF` · burn `#5A2A12` on the ply edge (6:1) · focus = 3px accent ring, offset 3px (ink ring on accent)

## Icon family
32 × 32 grid, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills, every icon carries one "plank" (a 4-unit-thick
rectangle) so the set reads as cut from the same sheet: step shelf, bridge, window perch, post, sisal coil,
fixings box, ply stack, parcel van, map pin, tape.

## Signature device
The plywood ledge: a pale top, a laminated EDGE strip that overhangs the panel by 14px each side, two triangular
brackets under it, and a burn mark on the edge (section name, or the price on a product). It repeats as: the hero
photo's shelf, the facts set out as planks of a rope bridge, the five products as a rising staircase of shelves,
the numbered "rises" of how an order runs, the planner's to-scale wall, the order form's tally shelf.
Home compositions, in order: split hero on a shelf · rope-bridge facts · rising staircase of priced shelves ·
icon ledger of what we make · live wall drawing (planner) · numbered rises + photo · two-column FAQ · tally/contact.

## Motion
One idea only: between ledges a trail of six paw prints steps in, one print every 160ms, once, when the path
scrolls into view. Runs only under `html.np-js` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; otherwise the prints
are simply there. Hamburger rungs fold to a cross. Buttons lift 2px on hover. Nothing loops.

## Voice
A woodworker who shares a flat with two climbers. Short sentences, real measurements, Australian spelling.
Never the b-word from the domain; no pet-care or cat-health advice; no load or safety ratings; no promises of proof.
