# Zarnay Yoga — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept (one sentence)
The whole site is one oak shelving unit seen face-on: every section is a shelf bay, the props stand on the
plank with a small paper price card leaning on them, and the bays left empty are the whitespace.

## Mood
quiet · warm · concrete

## Type
Display **Ibarra Real Nova** (400, 600, italic 400 — the italic is the "handwritten" price-card hand).
Body **Murecho** (400, 500, 700). Fallbacks are size-adjusted local faces so the swap does not shift layout.
Modular ratio 1.25 (major third), fluid:

| step | role | size |
|---|---|---|
| -1 | labels, card small print | clamp(0.8rem, 0.78rem + 0.1vw, 0.86rem) |
| 0 | body | clamp(1rem, 0.97rem + 0.15vw, 1.08rem) |
| 1 | lead, card name | clamp(1.2rem, 1.12rem + 0.35vw, 1.35rem) |
| 2 | h3 | clamp(1.4rem, 1.25rem + 0.6vw, 1.69rem) |
| 3 | h2 | clamp(1.8rem, 1.5rem + 1.3vw, 2.5rem) |
| 4 | page h1 | clamp(2.2rem, 1.7rem + 2.3vw, 3.3rem) |
| 5 | home h1 | clamp(2.35rem, 1.6rem + 3.4vw, 4.1rem) |

Body line-height 1.62, headings 1.08–1.2, measure capped at 66ch.

## Spacing scale (rem)
0.25 · 0.5 · 0.75 · 1 · 1.5 · 2.25 · 3.5 · 5 · 7 (bay head-room uses 5–7; an "empty bay" is 7+).

## Grid
The unit: max 1180 px, two oak uprights (12 px desktop, 6 px phone) as its side borders. Inside, a 12-column
grid with 24 px gutters (phone: 1 column, 20 px side padding). Every bay closes on a full-width plank
(14 px oak board + 3 px edge + soft under-shadow). Props are bottom-aligned so they touch the plank.

## Icon family ("thread line")
24 × 24 grid, 1.6 px stroke, round caps and round joins, no fills, 2 px corner radius on boxes, drawn for
this trade only: block, strap with D-rings, bolster, cushion, rolled mat, sack (set), parcel, needle and
thread, shears, window. One weight everywhere.

## Signature device
The prop on its plank with a leaning price card. It repeats with variation: the hero shelf (all six props),
the facts as brass-free shelf-edge labels, the featured shelf (two planks), cubby holes for the materials,
a painted sage bay for the Prop Finder, and one bay that is deliberately left empty to say what the shop
does not sell. Illustrated props are flat SVG in the palette; photographs are used for materials and the
workroom, framed like prints pinned to the back panel.

## Motion
One movement only: when a bay enters view each prop drops 6 px onto its plank, once
(transform only, 420 ms, slight overshoot, 60 ms stagger). Hamburger bars fold into a cross.
All of it is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion; nothing is hidden when scripts are off.

## Voice
A calm maker by the window: short declarative sentences, measurements, fabric names. Props are described
by what they are — height, firmness, fabric — and never by what happens to the person using them.
