Privacy at Zarnay Yoga

What zarnayyoga.com collects when you send an order, the single browser entry it stores, and how to ask us about your data.

Last updated November 26, 2026

Who is writing this

Zarnay Yoga is a small prop workroom in Bend, Oregon, USA. We run the website zarnayyoga.com to show six yoga props and to take orders for them. These notes say what the site learns about you, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. They are written by the people who answer the mailbox, in the same plain words.

What the order form collects

When you send the form on the order page, these details travel to us: your name, your e-mail, the shipping region, a street, city, state and ZIP if you typed them, the quantities and options you picked, the two totals the slip worked out, and whatever you wrote in the note. We use them for one job, which is to check your order, reply to you and, once you have paid, post the parcel. We ask for nothing else, and the page never asks for payment details.

How the form reaches us

FormSubmit carries the form for us. It is a go-between whose single job is posting typed fields to an inbox. It receives what you typed and forwards it as an e-mail to props@zarnayyoga.com. Our mailbox provider then stores that e-mail like any other. Payment happens later and elsewhere: through the secure link in our reply, on the payment company's own pages. Zarnay Yoga never sees or stores your card number.

The Prop Finder

The Prop Finder on the choosing page, and its short form on the home page, run entirely inside your browser. The height band, the practice style and any height you type stay on your device. They leave it only if you press the button that opens the order form, and then only as part of that page's address, to fill the form in for you.

Cookies and the one thing we store

This site sets no cookies of its own. On your first visit a small card asks about cookies; when you answer, the site writes a single entry named zy_cookies to your browser's local storage so the card does not come back. That entry records your choice and the date, and nothing reads it for any other purpose today. No analytics tool, advertising tag or tracking pixel is installed on zarnayyoga.com at launch. If we ever add an analytics tool, it will run only for visitors who chose "Accept all" or switched on "Analytics and performance", and this page will be updated first. You can erase the entry at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Our two typefaces arrive from Google Fonts. Opening a page therefore has your browser fetch font files from Google, exactly as happens on every other site set in hosted type.

What we never do

We do not sell your details, rent them or trade them. We do not build profiles, and we do not send newsletters; the only e-mails you get from us are replies about your order or your question.

How long we keep things

Order e-mails are kept for as long as we need them to deal with the order, any return and our ordinary record-keeping, and are then deleted. A question that never became an order is deleted within twelve months.

Your rights

It does not matter where you live: tell us and we will show you the order e-mails we keep under your name, fix them or erase them. Readers covered by the GDPR hold five named rights over those e-mails (access, correction, deletion, portability and objection) and may also take a complaint to the data protection authority of their own country. Californians have the CCPA set: to know what a business collects, to get it deleted, to opt out of the sale of personal information, and to be served the same whether or not they use those rights. On the sale point there is nothing to switch off, because Zarnay Yoga sells no data.

One e-mail to props@zarnayyoga.com is enough to use any of these. Please send it from the inbox your order came from so we can match it. A person replies by the next working day, and the work itself is finished inside thirty days.

Changes

If these notes change, the date at the top changes with them. The terms page sits beside this one, and the photograph credits are at the end of it.
