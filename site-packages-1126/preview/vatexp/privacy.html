Privacy

Privacy at VatExp.

Last updated November 26, 2026

Here is the short list of what vatexp.com finds out when you visit or order, the reason for each item, and the requests you are free to make of us. VatExp is a small indigo supply business in Portland, Maine, USA; the person reading your order is the same one packing it, and we would rather hold too little information than too much.

What reaches us with an order

An order form, once sent, hands us the items and quantities you chose, the shipping region, your name, your e-mail address, the delivery address you typed and anything you wrote in the message box. We use those details for one purpose: to confirm, pack, ship and follow up on that order, and to answer your questions about it.

FormSubmit, a relay that turns a web form into an e-mail, carries the form to our mailbox. Your entries arrive as one message after its own spam screening; it is the only outside party that handles what you type here. Payment happens later through a separate secure link, so this website never sees or stores your payment details.

Cookies and the consent note

vatexp.com sets no cookies of its own. When you answer the consent note, the site saves a single entry in your browser’s local storage under the name vx_cookie_ok, holding your choice and the date, so the note does not come back on every visit. For now, no other part of vatexp.com looks at that entry.

The site launches without a single script for analytics, advertising or tracking. If we ever add visitor measurement, it will stay switched off unless you have ticked “Analytics and performance” in the consent note, and this page will be updated first. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google’s servers.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, rent it, swap it, or share it for anyone else’s marketing. Nobody here builds a profile of you, and no newsletter goes out unless you asked to get one.

How long an order stays on file

An order e-mail stays in the mailbox while shipping questions, returns or our own records still depend on it; after that we delete it. Messages from people who asked a question and never ordered are cleared out before a year has passed.

What you can ask of us

Readers in the European Economic Area and the UK are covered by the GDPR. Under it you may ask for a copy of the personal data we hold (access), have mistakes fixed (correction), have the data erased (deletion), take it with you in a portable file (portability) and object to our use of it. Californians are covered by the CCPA: the right to know what was collected, the right to deletion, the right to opt out of a sale of personal information, which never takes place here, and the right not to be treated worse for asking.

Wherever you live, we will do the same for you. Write to dye@vatexp.com from the address you ordered with and say what you want; we act on it within thirty days and tell you when it is done.

Changes

Any edit to this page moves its “last updated” line to the day of the edit. Questions about privacy go to the same mailbox as everything else: dye@vatexp.com.
