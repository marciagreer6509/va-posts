# VatExp — art direction (written before any code)

**Concept, one sentence.** The whole site is one length of cotton lowered into an indigo vat: every section is one
dip darker than the one above it, and everything with a price hangs off the cloth on a punched cotton swatch tag.

**Mood, three words.** Patient · exact · stained.

## Type
- Display: Libre Caslon Display 400 (only weight; no faux bold anywhere). Body: Gowun Dodum 400.
- Modular ratio 1.25 (major third), 7 fluid steps:
  - step-1 `clamp(.84rem, .82rem + .1vw, .9rem)` — dip marks, tag labels, notes
  - step0 `clamp(1.02rem, .98rem + .2vw, 1.14rem)` — body, line-height 1.7
  - step1 `clamp(1.2rem, 1.12rem + .4vw, 1.42rem)` — lead paragraphs
  - step2 `clamp(1.45rem, 1.3rem + .75vw, 1.78rem)` — h3
  - step3 `clamp(1.75rem, 1.45rem + 1.5vw, 2.4rem)` — tag prices, h2 on inner bands
  - step4 `clamp(2.1rem, 1.6rem + 2.6vw, 3.2rem)` — h2
  - step5 `clamp(2.5rem, 1.5rem + 4.6vw, 4.5rem)` — h1
- Emphasis is done with the display face, size and the brass underline — never weight.
- Fallback faces are size-adjusted (`vxSerifHold`, `vxSansHold`) so swap does not shift layout.

## Spacing
8-point family: .5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 / 4 rem, plus a fluid band padding `clamp(4rem, 3rem + 5vw, 7.5rem)`.

## Grid
12 columns, 1180 px max cloth width, gutter `clamp(1.1rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)`. Body measure capped at 66ch.
Hamburger below 980 px. Tested to 360 px.

## Colour roles (assigned palette: theme #233A7A · accent #D8A23A · ink #10172E · ground #F6F2EA)
The dip ladder, top to foot — each band has a "wet" green it oxidises from, luminance-matched so AA holds on every frame:

| dip | ground | wet start | text | contrast (text / ground) |
|-----|--------|-----------|------|--------------------------|
| 0 raw cotton | #F6F2EA | — | ink #10172E | 15.9 |
| 1 | #E3EAF3 | #E4EDD2 | ink | 14.6 |
| 2 | #CBD8EC | #D0E0BE | ink | 12.1 |
| 3 | #AEC1E1 | #B6CFA4 | ink · soft #3B4566 (5.1) · theme (6.0) | 9.7 |
| 4 | #8AA2D2 | #93B78C | ink only | 6.9 |
| 5 | #4A66AE | #3F7556 | cotton #F6F2EA only | 4.95 (wet 4.8) |
| 6 | #233A7A | #173F38 | cotton 9.9 · pale #C9D3EC 7.4 · brass 4.8 (wet 5.0) |
| 7 | #182A5C | #133A35 | cotton · pale · brass | > 7 |
| 8 foot | #10172E | #0F2A26 | cotton · pale · brass | > 8 |

- surface (tags, form fields): #FBF8F1 with ink text (16+).
- line: #D9CFBB on cotton; `currentColor` hairlines at reduced strength via a lighter mixed colour, never opacity.
- accent (brass #D8A23A): button ground with ink text (7.6), eyelets, focus ring, links on dips 6-8.
- focus: 3 px brass outline + 2 px ink offset ring so it shows on every dip.
- De-emphasis is always a lighter/darker colour, never `opacity`.

## Icon family ("dye-room marks")
24 × 24 grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills except one small solid "drop" accent per icon,
2 px corner radius on boxes. Drawn for the trade: vat bucket, powder pouch, clamp-and-blocks, folded cloth,
refill drop, scale, thermometer, glove, parcel, return loop, thread spool.

## Signature device
1. **The dip ladder** — bands darken one dip at a time; each band carries a "Dip n" mark and ends on a wavy tide
   line where the dye stopped.
2. **Swatch tags** — every price (and the four facts) hangs on a cotton tag with a brass eyelet and a thread,
   tilted a degree or two, never in a plain price badge.
3. **The vat well** — a round top-down vat with its bronze "flower" drawn in SVG, used by the calculator.

Home page compositions (no two neighbours alike): split first screen with pinned photo + shade strip · a
clothesline of four fact tags · a shelf ledger of five products with one feature photo · the calculator well ·
a numbered two-column "how an order runs" · a three-up resist gallery · FAQ fold-outs · the foot with contact.

## Motion
One idea only: a band **oxidises** once when it enters the viewport — its ground starts at its wet green-yellow
and settles to its blue over 1.8 s (`@property --vxDip`, colour interpolation). Runs only under
`html.vxAwake` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; with JS off or reduced motion the band is simply blue.
Hamburger threads cross into an X (200 ms). Nothing else moves.
