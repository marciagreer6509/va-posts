Tray 02 · After you send

What happens after you send

  1. We read it.A person checks your order against the shelf, usually within hours and always within one working day.
  2. We e-mail the total.Items, delivery, and a secure payment link. If we spot a snag, such as a watch that won't fit the stand, we say so first.
  3. You pay, or you don't.Nothing has been charged up to this point. If you have changed your mind, leave the link alone; after a week we shelve the order.
  4. We test, pack and dispatch.Within one working day of payment, by tracked courier. Most parcels arrive two to four working days after you pay.

Where we deliver

We are in Singapore and deliver to Singapore addresses only: every mainland postal district and Sentosa. There is no collection point. Full costs and times are on the delivery page, and the 14-day returns terms are on the returns page.
