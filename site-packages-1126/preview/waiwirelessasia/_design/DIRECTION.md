# Wai Wireless Asia — art direction (written before any code)

**Concept, one sentence:** the whole site is a desk seen from directly above — the first screen is a planogram of a
clear desk, every product an outline drawn in plan view with a leader line to its name and price, and every later
section is a tray pulled out from under that desk's front edge.

**Mood:** exact · calm · handy.

## Type
Display **Bai Jamjuree** (600/700; engineered, squared — drawing-office labels). Body **Hind Vadodara** (400/500/600).
Modular scale, ratio 1.25 (major third), fluid:

| 1 | lead, slot titles | clamp(1.18rem, 1.1rem + 0.35vw, 1.36rem) |
| 2 | h3 | clamp(1.35rem, 1.2rem + 0.6vw, 1.70rem) |

Display line-height 1.04–1.15, tracking −0.015em; labels upper-case, +0.09em. Body line-height 1.62.

## Space
4-pt base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 112. Section rhythm = 72–112 between trays, 24–32 inside a slot.

## Grid
12 columns, 24 px gutters, 1240 px max; 16 px side gutter on phones. Body measure capped at 66ch.
Home first screen: 5 cols words / 7 cols desk plan; stacks under 1080 px (words first).

## Icon family — "plan view"
24 × 24 grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, 2 px corner radius, no fills except one accent dot per icon.
Every icon is the object seen from above (pad = two rings, stand = slab with three rings, lamp = base + arm + bar,
speaker = slab with grille dots, organiser = tray with a looped cable, mat = slab with a stitched edge).
The larger slot drawings are the same objects at 1 cm = 1 unit with a dimension line.

## Signature device
**The planogram.** Dark desk-mat grid, pale outlines, orange leader lines, name + price tags. It returns as:
(1) the home first screen, (2) slot drawings on every product row, (3) the planner's live desk drawing,
(4) dashed "empty slots" for the things we do not stock, (5) a ruler edge (cm ticks) dividing trays,
(6) a vertical leader line down the how-an-order-runs steps. Buttons are keycaps.

Home compositions (no neighbours alike): plan split → ruler facts strip → six-slot tray → dark planner bench →
photo + ledger rows → stepped leader column → dashed empty slots → offset FAQ → orange keycap band → contact tray.

## Motion
One thing moves: leader lines draw (stroke-dashoffset, 700 ms, 90 ms stagger, ease-out) once when a plan enters view.
Only under `html.dk-js`; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` shows them drawn with no transition. Keycaps press 1 px.
