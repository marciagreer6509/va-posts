Tray 03 · How an order runs

A small shop with one narrow job.

Wai Wireless Asia is a small online shop in Singapore, and it sells one narrow thing: gear that gets cables off the top of a desk. Every item is held in our own stock here, so what you order is already on a shelf near the packing bench when you order it.

The range is short because desks fill up with leads for only three reasons: charging, lighting and sound. Six products deal with those three, plus the kit that hides the leads nobody can get rid of. If a product needs a paragraph of warnings before it works, we leave it out.

An order starts with the form, or with the planner if you'd like a second opinion first. Nothing is charged when you send it. We check stock, add delivery and e-mail you the total with a secure payment link, normally the same working day.

Once that is paid, your order is picked and every item comes out of its box to be tested. Pads and stands charge a test phone. Lamps are run through all three colour temperatures and the dimmer. Speakers are paired and played. Each cable kit is counted, down to the last tie. Then the order is packed in a plain carton with paper padding, handed to the courier, and a tracking link is e-mailed to you.

What we care about is dull and useful: honest descriptions, real dimensions, and telling you when one pad is enough. What we turn away is listed in the next tray.
