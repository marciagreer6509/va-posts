# UFT Engineering — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept
Every page is a sheet from a drawing office: the visitor reads the shop the way a model engineer reads a drawing —
border, grid references, balloons, a parts list with prices, and a title block that says which sheet they are on.

## Mood
Exact · dry · workmanlike.

## Type
- Display: Oxanium (600/700/800) — squared, engineered; used for headlines, labels in caps, numerals in the parts list.
- Body: IBM Plex Sans Condensed (400/500/600) — drawing-office annotation feel, dense but readable.
- Scale: major third (1.25), base 17 px, 8 steps, fluid:
  - s-1 0.8rem (labels, grid refs, title-block captions, caps + 0.08em tracking)
  - s0 clamp(1rem, 0.96rem + 0.2vw, 1.0625rem) body
  - s1 clamp(1.15rem, 1.08rem + 0.35vw, 1.33rem) lead
  - s2 clamp(1.3rem, 1.18rem + 0.6vw, 1.66rem) h3
  - s3 clamp(1.55rem, 1.3rem + 1.2vw, 2.07rem) h2 small
  - s4 clamp(1.9rem, 1.45rem + 2.2vw, 2.6rem) h2
  - s5 clamp(2.3rem, 1.5rem + 3.9vw, 4.05rem) h1
  - s6 clamp(2.6rem, 1.4rem + 5.6vw, 5.06rem) price numerals / sheet numerals
- Line height 1.55 body, 1.02–1.1 display. Fallback stack size-adjusted (Arial Narrow / Arial) to hold layout while fonts swap.

## Spacing
4 px unit: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 112. Sections breathe at 72–112 px desktop, 48–72 px mobile.

## Grid
12 columns, 24 px gutters, sheet max width 1240 px inside a drawn border; body measure max 66ch.
The sheet border carries grid references: 1–8 along the top edge, A–F down the left edge (CSS-generated, never copy).

## Colour roles (contrast checked)
| role | value | pairs |
|---|---|---|
| ground (paper) | #EEF0EE | ink on ground 15.6:1 |
| surface (clean sheet) | #F8F9F7 | ink 16.6:1 |
| ink | #16191C | — |
| muted ink | #4A5259 | on ground 7.0:1, on surface 7.5:1 |
| theme (slate) | #3A4148 | ground text on theme 9.0:1; dark panels, header, title block |
| theme deep | #252B30 | ground text 12.6:1 |
| accent (marking amber) | #E3A90C | FILL only on light grounds with ink text (8.6:1); as text only on theme/deep (4.7:1 on theme at large/bold sizes, 6.6:1 on deep) |
| line | #8C959B | 1 px rules, witness lines (decorative) |
| light line on dark | #C9CFD2 | text on theme 6.6:1 |
| focus | 3 px accent outline + 2 px ink inner ring (visible on paper and on slate) |
De-emphasis is done with muted ink, never opacity.

## Icon family ("first-angle" set)
24 × 24 grid, 1.6 px stroke in currentColor, square caps, mitred joins, no fills except ONE amber datum mark per icon
(a 3 × 3 filled square or a short amber stroke) showing where the tool touches the work. Six icons: square, scriber +
punch, surface plate, parallels, toolmaker's clamp, kit tray.

## Signature device
The DIMENSION LINE. Every photograph is "dimensioned": witness lines leave its corners, a dimension line with
arrowheads spans it, and the label is a true spec of the goods (300, 200, 100, 75, 50 …). The same device underlines
headings (a short dimension with one arrowhead), measures the price in the parts list, and draws the progress of the
order steps. Paired with the TITLE BLOCK that closes every page (title, sheet n of 12, material: "paper", next step).
Balloons (circled item numbers with leader lines) tie product notes to the parts list.

## Home page compositions (no two adjacent alike)
1. Dimensioned split: oversized headline left, dimensioned photograph right.
2. General-notes band (slate, four numbered notes in one ruled row) — the facts strip.
3. Detail grid: six ballooned details, icon + two sentences + price, hairline cells sharing borders (no floating cards).
4. Tool panel (deep slate, amber numerals): live mini tap-drill read-out.
5. Two-column annotation: "how an order runs" as a numbered operation sheet beside a tall dimensioned photo.
6. Parts list table with prices (featured row).
7. FAQ as revision table (rev letter, question, answer).
8. Contact block + CTA as an oversized stamp line, then the title block.

## Motion
One motion only: dimension lines grow from their witness lines (transform scaleX/scaleY 0→1, 700 ms,
cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)) once, when 30 % in view (IntersectionObserver), only under html.js. Arrowheads and labels are
always visible. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → lines are drawn full length, no transition. Hover: links underline
in amber, buttons shift their amber datum tab by 2 px (transform only).
