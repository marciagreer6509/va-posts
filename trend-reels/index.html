What we know

  • Two completely different routes to millions of views. Barefoot wanderer is a REAL woman building a shipping-container house alone in the Australian bush, 60–90 s reels, filmed handheld. Juliaa Agusselaa is AI: one locked wide shot, the same blonde woman in a light-blue top and jeans, a yard or corner going from bare to finished in ~20 s.
  • Juliaa's reels carry NO voice at all, only music (see the Voice column). So an English voiceover over this format is open ground, and it fits what the Meta agent told you about RPM.
  • Barefoot wanderer talks in English, first person, almost non-stop: 16 of her top 30 have a full voiceover, typically ~360 words over a ~88 s reel (about 4 words a second). Her top 3 (22M, 4.5M, 3.8M) are all 90 s voiced recaps. Her opening line is a formula: "I've been building this container house alone and with no experience on a tiny budget of 100K for 17 months now…" Voiced reels: median 2.2M views vs 1.8M without a voice.
  • Juliaa's topics are narrow: front/side-yard makeovers (paths with stepping stones, rock beds, flower borders, solar lights) plus a few indoor corners and wall-crack repairs. Titles follow one pattern: "From Plain Grass To A Dreamy Garden Oasis", "Never Throw Away … Until You See This".
  • Juliaa only went AI-viral recently: 1M+ reels started Feb 2026, she posts 2–3 a day (79 posts so far in Nov), and the 32M top reel is from 17 Sep. Most 1M+ reels go up 7–10 AM US ET.
  • Barefoot wanderer posts rarely (3–8 a month), almost always 3–5 PM US ET, and every one of her 171 reels has passed 100K. None of her reels from the last 30 days has passed 1M yet.
