What happens next

  1. We answer within one working dayYou get photos of the exact pots pulled from the shelf for you, and the total with Original Delivery.
  2. You look, then decideApprove them, or ask us to swap a piece for a sibling from the same batch.
  3. You pay by secure linkThat e-mail holds a link to pay securely. Your card is untouched until you click it.

Pressing send asks for pots; it does not pay for them. Your card stays untouched until you have looked at the photos and said yes.

Where we are

Fallen Dragon Studios packs every order in Taos, New Mexico, USA. There is no showroom; the studio is a working pottery, and the site is the shop.
