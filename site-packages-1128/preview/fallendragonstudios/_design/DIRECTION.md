# Fallen Dragon Studios — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept, in one sentence
The site is a small kiln opened face-on: every section is a kiln shelf (a thick fired-clay slab on
buff kiln posts) with the pieces standing on it, the heat still glowing behind, and the studio's runny
turquoise glaze — Rocket Pool — dripping over a slab edge the way it does when a glaze fires too generously.

## Mood, in three words
Earthy. Vivid. Unhurried.

## Who the first screen is for
People who want a daily mug, bowl, plate or planter that was thrown by hand and exists once.
They get: the piece, glazed in a small batch, shipped double-boxed inside the USA ("Original Delivery").
Primary step: **Order stoneware**. Secondary: the Planter Pot Sizer and "How an order runs".

## Type
- Display: **Cinzel Decorative** 400/700 (inscriptional capitals; lower case renders as plain capitals, so
  headings are written in sentence case and read as a flourished initial + plain caps). Used ONLY for h1, h2,
  the nav, the seal, the CTA and the price tiles. Never for running text; headings kept under ~32 characters.
- Text: **Castoro** 400 / 400 italic / 700. Sub-heads (h3/h4) are Castoro, not Cinzel, so pages do not shout.
- Scale: modular, ratio ≈ 1.25 (major third), seven fluid steps
  - `s-1` 0.875rem (small print)
  - `s0`  clamp(1.0625rem, 1rem + 0.25vw, 1.1875rem)  body, line-height 1.62
  - `s1`  clamp(1.25rem, 1.12rem + 0.55vw, 1.5rem)   lead, h3
  - `s2`  clamp(1.5rem, 1.25rem + 1.1vw, 1.95rem)    section sub-title
  - `s3`  clamp(1.7rem, 1.3rem + 2vw, 2.6rem)        h2
  - `s4`  clamp(1.95rem, 1.2rem + 3.5vw, 3.35rem)    h1
  - `s5`  clamp(2.6rem, 1.4rem + 5.4vw, 4.75rem)     tool result numerals
- Fallbacks are size-adjusted `@font-face` aliases of local Georgia so the swap does not move the layout.

## Spacing scale
0.5 · 0.75 · 1 · 1.5 · 2.25rem, then two fluid steps for section rhythm:
`g6` clamp(2.75rem, 2rem + 3vw, 4.5rem) and `g7` clamp(3.5rem, 2.4rem + 5vw, 6.5rem).

## Grid
One bay, max 1200px, centred. A kiln post (clamp 8–22px) on each side; content gutter clamp(1.1rem, 4vw, 2.75rem)
inside the posts. Twelve implied columns with 24px gaps on desktop; running text never wider than 66ch.

## Icon family ("everything stands on a shelf")
48 × 48 grid · 2.25px ink stroke · round caps and joins · each icon is a side view standing on a clay-coloured
shelf line at y = 42 · exactly one flat turquoise glaze shape per icon with a wavy lower edge (the glaze line) ·
no emoji, no icon font, drawn for this trade (mug, bowl, plate on edge, planter + saucer, serving bowl,
four-piece stack, glaze bucket, wheel, loop tool, kiln, bent cone, box in a box, drainage hole).

## Signature device — the kiln shelf
1. **Slab**: a thick fired-clay bar under every section (lit top face, shaded underside, grog speckle).
2. **Posts**: buff kiln posts on both sides of each bay, so the page stacks like kiln furniture.
3. **Glow**: a radial heat gradient rising from the slab behind the pieces.
4. **Glaze run**: a turquoise run that pools on a slab and drips over its edge — a different run on each shelf.
5. **Chop + tiles**: prices are pressed into small bisque tiles; the FD chop seal is the logo, centred.
Variation down the page: brick-arch kiln mouth (hero) → arch-topped pieces on two tiers → dark "inside the bowl"
band with the pooled glaze in a circle → icon ledger on kiln wash → tool panel → firing-curve timeline →
question shelf → clay-coloured closing band → kiln-floor footer.

## Header (assigned): centred seal, split menu
Round FD chop dead centre; two links each side (Mugs & bowls · Planters & serving | Pot sizer · About);
wordmark far left, "Order stoneware" far right. Under 1100px the links fold into a sheet opened by the word
"Studio" set under the seal — a small glaze drip under the word stretches when the sheet is open. Never three bars.

## Motion
One idea only: the glow behind a shelf warms from ash grey to ember once (1.6s ease) when the shelf enters view.
Plus state changes that answer the visitor (sheet, question shelf, form). Everything is off under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; with JavaScript off every shelf is simply lit.

## Voice
A potter with clay under the nails who enjoys a glaze accident. Plain verbs, concrete sizes, trade words used
correctly (trimming, foot ring, bisque, cone 6, reduction, pooling, crawl, celadon, tenmoku). No health or
safety ratings, no certificates, no invented people, dates or numbers.
