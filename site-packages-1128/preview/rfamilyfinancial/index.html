How we work

From your note to your front door

R Family Keepsakes is a small bindery in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. We make six things and only six: a family history record book, a recipe binder, a baby memory book, a summer journal for children, an archival keepsake box and a family tree wall chart. They are the things we kept wishing someone made properly, with paper that does not yellow, thread instead of glue, and enough blank space for a family that will not fit a form.

  1. You send the order form

    Choose what you want and how many, say where it is going and whether it is a gift. Nothing is charged at this point.

  2. We answer by e-mail

    By the next working day the bindery writes back saying what is on the shelf, the full total with shipping, and a link to pay safely.

  3. We check and wrap

    Each book is opened flat and checked page by page for a stray thread or a creased corner, then wrapped in tissue, kraft paper and cotton tape.

  4. It leaves by Original Delivery

    Paid orders leave within two working days in a rigid carton, tracked, and usually arrive three to six working days later.

  5. You write in it

    Pencil first, then ink. If anything about the parcel disappoints you, the returns page says plainly what happens next.

What we care about

We would sooner sew slowly than glue quickly. A sewn book opens flat on a kitchen table and stays open while your hands are full; a glued one snaps shut and, in a few years, lets go of its pages. We keep the list short so that every item is on the shelf or a few days from it, and we leave every cover blank, because a cover that says nothing suits every branch of a family.

What we turn down

  • We do not research family history for clients. The planner counts your boxes; filling them is your pleasure, not our business.
  • We do not print your photographs. The books carry corner mounts, so prints made anywhere slip in and out.
  • We do not offer personalized embossing. No names, initials or dates are stamped on any cover.
  • We do not ship outside the USA.

The family tree planner

Plan your tree before you buy

A family tree grows faster than people expect: two parents, four grandparents, eight great-grandparents, and by the seventh generation back there are 128 people in a single row.

So count before you choose a chart. The planner uses the standard pedigree rule: counting the child as one, n generations back needs 2n + 1 − 1 boxes. Four generations back is 31 boxes; five is 63. Try it on the facing page, then open the full planner to add brothers and sisters, see which record book holds everyone, and carry the pick into the order form.

Questions, answered on slips

What people ask before they order

What paper are the books made of?

Acid-free, lignin-free text paper with a mild alkaline buffer, in a warm cream instead of bright white. The record book and the baby memory book use an 80 lb sheet that takes pencil, fountain pen and archival pigment pens without showing through; the recipe cards are a stiffer 110 lb index stock. The fore-edge of the record book is torn against a steel rule, which leaves the soft deckle edge you can feel with a thumb.

Can I add pages later?

To the recipe binder, yes. Rings exist for exactly this, and any A5 six-hole card or page fits ours. The record book and the baby memory book are sewn, so their page count is fixed: 128 record pages in the one, 80 pages in the other. If a family outgrows a record book, a second volume matches the first, and loose extras live happily in a keepsake box.

What is the Rocket Pool summer journal?

A wire-bound memory journal for one child and one summer, at $24. We named it Rocket Pool for the two things every child puts in a summer: rockets in the yard and days at the pool. Inside are ten weeks of short prompts, a page for drawing the rocket, a page of pool rules the child would make, and a pocket for ticket stubs. It suits writers and scribblers from about five to eleven.

What does Original Delivery include?

Original Delivery is our standard shipping, and it is gift-wrapped as a matter of course: acid-free tissue, kraft paper, cotton tape and a plain card. It costs $7 per order to the 48 contiguous states and $14 to Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, leaves the bindery within two working days of payment, and is tracked to the door.

Can I order a gift for someone else?

Yes. Put their name and address in the delivery fields of the order form and tick the gift box; we leave every price out of the parcel. Give us up to forty words and we write them by hand on the card, legibly, which is more than can be said for most family recipe cards.

Can I return a book?

Yes. Any book nobody has written in may come back inside 30 days of reaching you. Send it back in its carton, at your cost if you changed your mind and at ours if we sent something faulty or wrong. The refund goes to your original payment method within five working days once the book is back on our bench. The returns page gives the whole policy in about a minute.
