# R Family Keepsakes — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept, in one sentence
The website IS the family album: every section is a spread of an open cloth-bound book lying on a linen
tablecloth — two facing pages with a centre-gutter shadow, photographs held by photo corners, and one
rust ribbon marker that drops onto each spread and stops at the price.

## Mood — three words
Heirloom · hand-bound · wry.

(The voice is a bookbinder who has a drawer of her grandmother's recipe cards: gentle, specific, quietly
funny. Never cute, never sentimental; the jokes are dry and short, the specifications are exact.)

## Layout archetype: OPEN ALBUM
- A "spread" (`rf-spread`) = a forest-green cover board that peeks out all round, two paper pages
  (`rf-spread__page`), a gutter shadow where they meet, page-edge hairlines on the outer sides, a folio
  number in each outer bottom corner (CSS counters, so no stray digits in the text).
- `rf-spread--left` / `rf-spread--right` say which page carries the photographs; they alternate down a
  content page so the eye zig-zags the way it does through an album.
- Under 860 px a spread becomes ONE right-hand page: spine and gutter shadow on the left, the two pages
  stacked, a dashed fold line between them. You read one page at a time, as on a phone you would.
- Sections "turn like pages": every spread ends with a curled lower-right corner and its folio.

## Section rhythm on the home page (no two neighbours share a composition)
1. Title-page spread — words left, frontispiece plate right, long ribbon with the price.
2. Endpaper band — full-bleed green "endpaper" with four pasted bookplates (the facts strip).
3. Album spread — six mounted prints in a mirrored 1-large + 2-small arrangement, a small ribbon on each.
4. Colophon leaf — one wide leaf: how an order runs, set on a sewing thread with five stations, bench print.
5. Planner spread — rule in words left, live fan chart right, ribbon carries the running total.
6. Contents leaf — narrow single leaf, six icon entries with dotted leaders running to the price.
7. Slips — the FAQ as six ruled index slips, fanned, one open at a time.
8. Back cover — green cloth band, an inset print with cream corners, the value line and the ribbon button.
9. Imprint strip — mailbox, hours, city and service area.

## Signature device
THE RIBBON MARKER. Rust satin, swallow-tailed, always vertical, always hanging from the head of a spread,
always ending on a price (or on the running total in the planner and the order form). Variations: long on
the title page, short on each album print, as the hanging "Order keepsakes" tab in the masthead, as the
swallow-tailed primary button. Supporting cast, never competing: green photo corners on every photograph,
the gutter shadow, the double rule of the masthead.

## Header (assigned): STACKED MASTHEAD
Centred sprig mark → centred wordmark in IM Fell English → the tagline in small tracked capitals → a thin
double rule → the nav centred on its own line, like the title page of a book. The "Order keepsakes" CTA is
a rust ribbon tab hanging from the rule at the end of the nav line. On mobile the nav folds away behind a
paper tab labelled "Contents" that hangs from the rule (beside the ribbon tab) and unfolds as a contents
list with dotted leaders and roman folios. No bars anywhere.

## Type
Display: IM Fell English 400 + italic (headlines, prices, captions, bookplates).
Body: Merriweather 400 / 700 / italic 400 (text, labels in tracked capitals).
Modular ratio 1.25 (major third), fluid:
| step | role | size |
|---|---|---|
| -1 | labels, folios, captions | 0.8125rem |
| 0 | body | clamp(1rem, .965rem + .17vw, 1.0625rem) |
| 1 | lead | clamp(1.125rem, 1.08rem + .22vw, 1.25rem) |
| 2 | h4 / entry titles | clamp(1.3rem, 1.2rem + .5vw, 1.5625rem) |
| 3 | h3 | clamp(1.55rem, 1.35rem + .95vw, 1.953rem) |
| 4 | h2 | clamp(1.9rem, 1.5rem + 1.9vw, 2.441rem) |
| 5 | h1 | clamp(2.2rem, 1.45rem + 3vw, 3.052rem) |
| 6 | ribbon price, big numerals | clamp(2.5rem, 1.3rem + 5.2vw, 3.815rem) |
Body line-height 1.75, headings 1.08–1.15. Fallback faces are size-adjusted (measured against the metric
twin of Times): Fell 101.8 %, Fell italic 86.5 %, Merriweather 119.5 %, Merriweather bold 115.4 % — so
the swap does not move the page.

## Spacing scale (rem)
0.25 · 0.5 · 0.75 · 1 · 1.5 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 6 · 8. Page padding clamp(1.4rem, 3.6vw, 3.25rem). Spreads
are separated by clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 5.5rem) of bare tablecloth.

## Grid
Book block max 74rem (1184 px), side gutters clamp(.75rem, 2.6vw, 2rem). A spread is 2 equal pages; the
text measure inside a page tops out near 62ch. Single leaves: wide 60rem, narrow 46rem. Tables scroll
inside their own frame under 560 px.

De-emphasis is done with the muted ink colour, never with opacity. Gradients and textures live on
pseudo-elements only, so every text node sits on a flat, measurable colour.

## Icon family — "bindery line"
48 × 48 grid, 1.75 px stroke in the theme green, round caps and joins, 2 px corner radii, drawn slightly
by hand (no perfect symmetry), no fills — except ONE small rust element in every icon (a ribbon tail, a
thread, a flame, a bow). Icons: record book, ring binder, baby book, rocket over water, lidded box, wall
chart, needle and thread, deckle sheet, wrapped parcel, branching tree, envelope, clock, pin, pencil.
The sprig mark (stem, seven leaves) is the only filled drawing and appears in the masthead, the favicon,
the bookplates and as the section fleuron.

## Motion
One animation, the assigned one: when a spread enters the viewport its ribbon slides down into place,
once (transform only, 900 ms, a soft overshoot, 60 ms stagger between ribbons on one spread). Hover:
prints lift 2 px, the ribbon button's tail lengthens. The Contents tab unfolds its list in 280 ms.
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → ribbons are simply in place, nothing moves, smooth scroll is off.
With JavaScript off nothing is hidden: ribbons in place, FAQ open, nav visible, no consent banner.

## Photographs
Real photographs only (stock API), each shown as a mounted print: white deckle border, four green photo
corners, a tilt of under 1.5°, an italic caption numbered as a plate. Pairs chosen so a product reads as
one object: the green cloth book closed (title page) and open (album); the cream cloth book for the baby
memory book; punched cards beside a chestnut binder; a wire-bound cream journal; a pale gray archival box.

## The tool
FAMILY TREE PLANNER — generations back (2–7), siblings yes/no → boxes by the pedigree rule (2ⁿ⁺¹ − 1),
couples (2ⁿ − 1), pages needed, which chart layout, how many record books, the total; a live fan chart;
every rule printed beside it; a button that carries the pick into the order form by URL parameters.

## Favicon / social card
Favicon: forest-green tile, a cream roman R from the display face, a rust ribbon down the right edge.
Social card: the title-page spread in miniature — wordmark, tagline, ribbon.
