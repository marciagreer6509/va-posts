    After you press send

    Once your note reaches the bindery

    1. Someone at the bench reads it. Your order lands in the bindery mailbox and is opened at the bench, usually the same working day.
    2. The total comes back to you. By the next working day the bindery writes with what is on the shelf, the full total with shipping, and a safe link for paying. Your card is untouched until then.
    3. You pay by the link. It opens the payment provider’s own page. This website never sees a card number.
    4. We wrap and send. The parcel goes by Original Delivery inside two working days, with its tracking number e-mailed that same day.

    We are in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, USA, and ship to all 50 states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico; we do not ship anywhere else. A question before you order is welcome at books@rfamilyfinancial.com. The full detail is on the shipping and gift wrap page and the returns and replacements page.
