Terms

The terms, kept short

These are the terms for using rfamilyfinancial.com, the website of R Family Keepsakes, a bindery in Lancaster, Pennsylvania.

What the site is for

The site describes six goods, shows their prices in US dollars, offers a family tree planner you may use as often as you like, and carries an order form. That is all it does.

Orders

Sending the order form is an inquiry, not a contract. A sale exists only once we have replied by e-mail to say the goods are available, stated the total, and received your payment through the link in that reply. Before that point neither the bindery nor you owes the other anything. The prices on the site are the prices we charge; if a page and our reply ever disagree, we honor the lower figure.

The planner

The family tree planner does arithmetic on the numbers you give it. It is a counting aid, not research into anybody’s ancestry, so check its pick against your own tree before you order.

Fair use of the site

Please do not send the form in someone else’s name without their knowledge, flood it with automated submissions, or copy the site wholesale. Ordinary browsing, linking and quoting with credit are welcome.

Who owns what

The words, drawings, page designs and the planner belong to R Family Keepsakes. The photographs are used under the licenses listed in the image credits below and belong to their makers. The family history you write in our books belongs, of course, to you.

Limits

We describe the goods as accurately as we can, and the returns page says what we do when something is wrong. Beyond the remedies set out there, and as far as the law allows, our responsibility for any order stops at the amount you paid for it. We are not responsible for indirect losses, such as a missed occasion when a carrier runs late.

Governing law

The law of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania governs these terms, and any dispute belongs before the courts that sit in Lancaster County.

Changes

The bindery may rewrite these terms from time to time. Whatever wording stood here when you sent your order is the wording your order keeps.
