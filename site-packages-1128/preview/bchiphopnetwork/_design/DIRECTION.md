# BC Hip Hop — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept, in one sentence
The whole site is a practice spot seen from directly above: a ring of interlocking floor panels with the crew
standing round it, and everything the shop sells — floors, gear, the planner, the Rocket Pool split — is drawn on
that floor in plan view and labelled with strips of yellow floor tape.

## Mood, three words
Overhead · taped · loud.

## Who the first screen is for
A b-boy, b-girl or crew captain in British Columbia who is tired of practising on bare concrete. In five seconds
they must read: this is for breakers and crews · it sells floors you can lay anywhere · "Order a floor" · or
"Plan your floor" first.

## Type
- Display: **Rubik Mono One** — ultra-wide monospaced caps, 0.85 em per glyph. Always upper case, weight 400,
  tracking −0.02 em, line-height 1.06. Used for H1/H2/H3, index numbers, the wordmark and every price.
  Because each glyph is 0.85 em wide, display lines are written short (≤ 14 glyphs on a phone).
- Body: **Pathway Extreme** (variable: opsz 8–144, wdth 75–100, wght 300–800). Running text at width 100,
  weight 400; labels and tape text at **width 75, weight 700–800, caps, +0.05 em** ("stencil on tape").
- Scale: modular, ratio 1.25 at desktop, fluid with clamp():

  | step | role                       | clamp()                                   | px at 390 → 1366 |
  |------|----------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------|
  | −1   | labels, tape, fine print   | clamp(0.8125rem, 0.79rem + 0.1vw, 0.875rem) | 13 → 14        |
  | 0    | body                       | clamp(1rem, 0.97rem + 0.16vw, 1.0625rem)    | 16 → 17        |
  | 1    | lede                       | clamp(1.125rem, 1.05rem + 0.34vw, 1.3125rem)| 18 → 21        |

- Fallbacks carry size-adjust / ascent / descent overrides measured from the two TTFs, so the swap does not move
  the layout.

## Spacing scale
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 104 · 144 px. Section padding = clamp(56px, 8vw, 120px).

## Grid
12 columns, gutter clamp(16px, 2.2vw, 28px), lane 1180px, side padding clamp(20px, 5vw, 48px).
Body measure 36em ≈ 66–70 characters in Pathway Extreme (its zero is wide, so `ch` is not used).

## Icon family — "plan view"
48 × 48 grid, 2.5 px stroke in currentColor, round caps and joins, 3 px corner radius, everything drawn as if
looking straight down at it; each icon carries exactly ONE tape-yellow filled shape. Drawn for this trade:
rolled floor, four panels, cypher circle, sling bag, beanie from above, ring split into shares, spray + cloth,
taped parcel, tape measure.

## Signature device
**The cypher ring, seen from above.** Twelve interlocking floor panels in a ring; the headline sits in the open
middle; three round photographs stand on the ring like crew members; prices are printed on yellow tape strips
stuck across it. It returns down the page with variation:
1. first screen — the full ring, turning a quarter turn into place;
2. floors — each floor is a photograph cut to the floor's own shape (square, four tiles, circle), all at one scale;
3. planner — the ring becomes a dial cut into one wedge per crew member: the Rocket Pool share;
4. inner pages — a quarter of the ring holds the page number (01–07, the same numbers as the Menu index);
5. corners — quarter-circle photo crops and section corners; 6. footer, 404, thanks, favicon, social card.
Tape strips (yellow, torn ends, a few degrees off square) carry every price and label.

## Home page section rhythm (no two neighbours share a composition)
ring stage (radial) → tape line (one thin yellow band) → three floors at one scale (bottom-aligned shapes) →
gear rows (split 5/7, stacked rows with round photos) → planner (night band, text + dial) → six panels with icons
(2 × 3 on a quarter ring) → how an order runs (dust band, wide photo + numbered arc) → questions (narrow column,
accordion) → call to action (tape-yellow band, half ring) → reach us (three columns) → baseboard (ink).

## Header (assigned)
A thin top line: text-only wordmark (display face) left, the word "Menu" right, the "Order a floor" chip beside it,
the tagline between them (on a phone the tagline rides on a tape strip above the line and scrolls away).
"Menu" opens a full-screen index over night: 01 Floors · 02 Cypher gear · 03 Floor planner · 04 About ·
05 Shipping · 06 Returns · 07 Order a floor, each a large numbered link. No bars, no icon.

## Motion
- Ring: rotate −90° → 0° once on load, 1.1 s, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); the round photos counter-rotate so they stay
  upright while they travel. Nothing loops.
- Menu index: circular wipe from the word "Menu" (clip-path circle), 0.45 s; links rise 12 px in a 40 ms stagger.
- Buttons: hard shadow shortens on hover (2 px shift). Tape straightens on hover.
- Planner dial and totals: instant (numbers must never lag behind the inputs).
- prefers-reduced-motion: every animation and transition off, scroll-behaviour auto.
- Motion classes only apply under html.cy-js, so nothing is hidden when scripts are off.
