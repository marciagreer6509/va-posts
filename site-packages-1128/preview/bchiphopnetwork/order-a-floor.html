Once the form is in

The next four steps

  1. Someone here looks it over

    We check your items against what is on the shelf and price Original Delivery for your province. An automatic note lands in your inbox straight away, so you know the form arrived.

  2. You get your total

    Inside one working day an e-mail lands with the whole breakdown: items, delivery, the total and, for a crew, every Rocket Pool share, plus a secure link to pay. Your card stays untouched until you use that link.

  3. We pack and ship

    Once paid, the parcel is out of our Vancouver shop inside two working days, and its tracking code goes to that same inbox.

  4. You roll it out

    The floor arrives rolled and ready to lay. Changed your mind before paying? Do nothing: an unpaid request simply lapses after seven days.
