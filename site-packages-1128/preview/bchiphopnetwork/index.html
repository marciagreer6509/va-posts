How we work

How an order runs

BC Hip Hop is a small online shop in Vancouver that sells one kind of thing: floors to break on, and the gear that goes with them. The idea is plain. Concrete eats sweaters and grass does not spin, so a crew needs a surface it can carry to the spot, lay in a couple of minutes and roll up again when the session ends.

We keep the range short on purpose. Three floors cover most of what breakers ask us for: a one-piece roll-out for power moves, a pack of panels for footwork in tight rooms, and a 3 m cypher circle for a full crew. Three pieces of gear look after them. Every item has one price, printed on every page it appears on, and Original Delivery is charged once per order however many pieces are in it.

What we care about is that the floor suits the way you actually practise. That is why the floor planner exists, and why we would sooner answer five questions by e-mail than send a circle to a crew whose garage is two and a half metres wide. If the planner says a floor will not fit, believe it.

And there are things we leave to others. This is a shop for floors and gear, nothing else:

  • No classes or lessons
  • No battles or events
  • No music or beats for sale
  • No shipping outside Canada

  1. Plan

    Run the floor planner, or e-mail us your crew size and the space you have. We will tell you which floor we would pick and why.

  2. Send the form

    Choose your floor and gear on the order page, add where it is going and send it. Nothing is charged on the site.

  3. Confirm

    Within one working day we e-mail your total, with Original Delivery for your province, and a secure payment link.

  4. Dispatch

    Paid orders leave Vancouver within two working days. Your tracking number arrives the day the parcel ships.

  5. Roll out

    The floor arrives rolled. Let it lie flat for a few minutes, tape the seam if it is a circle, and throw down.

Asked before buying

Questions crews ask us

The roll-out, 2 × 2 m. It is one sheet with no join, so there is nothing for a shoulder or a hip to catch on in a windmill or a backspin, and its top layer is fast enough to keep a spin turning. The cypher circle is the same vinyl with one taped seam down the middle and takes power too; it is simply more floor than one or two dancers need. Panels have joins inside the square. You feel them under a spinning back, so keep panels for footwork, toprock and freezes.

Yes. Smooth, dry concrete is the best base there is. Sweep it first, because grit trapped under vinyl prints through and scratches the underside. Lay the floor top side up, give it a few minutes to relax flat, and roll it up when you finish instead of leaving it out in sun or rain. Flat asphalt and paving work as well. Grass, gravel and sand do not: a floor wants something hard and even beneath it, or it wrinkles the moment you step on.

The roll-out floor is about 9 kg and rolls to a tube 2 m long, which one person can shoulder. A pack of four panels is about 10 kg in a flat 75 cm stack with its own carry strap. The cypher circle is about 16 kg, split into two rolls of roughly 8 kg, so two people take a roll each. The padded carry bag holds one rolled floor, or both halves of a circle side by side.

A Rocket Pool is our name for a crew order: one floor, bought together. One member fills in the order form and enters the crew size, and the form shows each member's share beside the total. We e-mail a single payment link to the person who ordered, the crew squares up between themselves, and the floor ships to one address with one Original Delivery charge. The floor planner works out the same split before you order, so nobody gets a surprise.

Original Delivery is our standard tracked service. Orders leave Vancouver within two working days of payment, then take 3–6 working days inside British Columbia, 4–7 to Alberta and up to 15 to the territories. The shipping page lists every region with its cost and its time, and a tracking number reaches you by e-mail the day your order ships.

Yes, within 30 days of delivery. Unroll it indoors, check the size, stand on it in socks: all fine. A floor that has been danced on outside cannot come back as new, so decide before the first session. Return postage is on you, except when the floor turned up faulty or banged up; then it is on us. Your money lands back where it came from, inside five working days of the floor reaching our Vancouver shop again. The returns page has the detail.
