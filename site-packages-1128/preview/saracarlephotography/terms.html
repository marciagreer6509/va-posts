What this site is for

saracarlephotography.com is the website of Sara Carle Photography, a print room in Bradford, West Yorkshire, England. It describes what we make — photo prints, mounted and framed prints, canvas wraps and lay-flat photo books, all produced from the customer’s own photographs — gives our prices in pounds sterling, offers a print size calculator, and carries the form through which orders reach us.

How an order is formed

Sending the order form is a request, not a purchase. It tells us what you would like; it does not bind you to buy and it does not bind us to supply. A contract exists only when we have checked your files, e-mailed you the confirmed total with a secure payment link, and you have paid through that link.

The six prices on our list are fixed. Anything outside the list is priced in our reply, and you are free to say no to it. The running total on the order form is arithmetic on the quantities you entered and is confirmed, or corrected, in that same reply. Postage, dispatch times, returns and refunds are set out on the postage and packaging and returns and reprints pages, which form part of these terms.

We may turn an order down, for instance when a file cannot be printed properly at the size requested and you would rather not change the size.

Your photographs

You keep every right in the photographs you send us. By sending them you confirm that they are yours to have printed, or that you have the permission of whoever owns them, and you allow us to copy them for one purpose: making your order. We will not print material that is unlawful to reproduce.

Fair use of the site

Use the site to read about our work, to run the calculator and to place orders. Please do not try to break it, load it with automated requests, send anything unlawful through the form, or pass yourself off as someone else when you write to us.

What belongs to us

The wording, layout, drawings and the calculator on this site belong to Sara Carle Photography. You may quote from the site or link to it; you may not copy it wholesale or present it as your own. The photographs used as illustration belong to their photographers and are credited at the foot of this page.

Where our responsibility stops

We take care that what is written here is right, but the site is offered as it stands and the calculator’s answers are a guide worked from a standard rule, not a promise about any particular print. If we get an order wrong, our responsibility is to reprint it or refund what you paid for it, as the returns page describes. We are not responsible for losses that could not reasonably have been foreseen when the order was confirmed.

None of this takes away rights the law gives you as a consumer, and nothing here limits our responsibility where the law says it cannot be limited.

Governing law

These terms, and any order made under them, are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction over any dispute. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland you may also bring a claim in your local courts.

We may revise these terms from time to time. The version on this page on the day we confirm your order is the one that applies to it. Questions go to prints@saracarlephotography.com.
