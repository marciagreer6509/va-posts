Roll 08Returns and reprints

If it is wrong, we print it again.

Everything we make is made to order from your own photograph, so it can never go back on a shelf. We take it back all the same. This page says how, who pays the postage and how fast the money returns.

The rulesFour cases

What we do in each case.

Within 30 days of delivery

Faulty, damaged or not what you ordered

E-mail us a photo of the problem. We reprint the item and post it by Original Delivery at our cost, or refund you in full, including the postage you paid — whichever you prefer.

Keep the faulty item until we reply. Usually we do not need it back; if we do, we send a prepaid label, so the return costs you nothing.

Within 14 days of delivery

A change of mind

Tell us, then send the item back unused, undamaged and in the packing it arrived in. You pay the return postage and the parcel is yours to look after until it reaches us, so use a tracked service.

We refund the price of the item once it is back. The original postage is not refunded on a change of mind.

Within 14 days of delivery

An exchange

If you would rather have a different size, the other paper or another moulding, return the first one as for a change of mind. We make the new one and charge or refund the difference in price, plus Original Delivery on the new parcel.

Within 5 working days

How and when the refund is made

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through the same secure payment provider. For a fault that means within five working days of our agreeing it; for a change of mind, within five working days of the item reaching us. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

Fair warningWhat is not a fault

Three things a reprint will not fix.

None of these stops you sending a print back as a change of mind within 14 days. They are simply not faults, so the free reprint does not apply.

  • Softness we warned you about. We check every file before printing. If we told you a photo was short of pixels for the size and you asked us to go ahead, the print is as sharp as the file allows.
  • A crop you approved. When the paper shape trims more than a tenth of a picture we e-mail the crop first. Once you have said yes to it, that is the print.
  • The difference between a screen and paper. A screen is lit from behind and paper is not, so a print always looks a shade quieter than the phone it came from. We print to the colour profile in your file.
