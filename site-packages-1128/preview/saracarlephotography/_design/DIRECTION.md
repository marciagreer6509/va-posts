# Sara Carle Photography — art direction (written before any code)

## Concept, in one sentence
The whole site is a photographer's contact sheet lying on a light table: the brand runs up a film-edge
rail, every section is a strip of numbered frames, prices are set in the strip's edge print, and a red
grease-pencil ring marks the frame worth printing.

## Mood, in three words
Exact · inky · warm.

## Who it is for / what the first screen must say
Anyone with photographs on a phone or a camera who wants them on paper. First screen, 390 px and 1366 px:
"Your photos, printed and framed as they are" + what we make + where it is posted from + **Order prints** +
"Check how big your photo prints" (the tool) + the six prices as six frames.

## Type
Display **Anybody** (variable: width 50–150, weight 400–900) — reads like the lettering on a film box.
Body **Hubot Sans** (400 / 500 / 600) — plain, slightly technical, easy at small sizes.

Three voices, never more:
- *Slate* (headlines): Anybody 800, width 100 %, sentence case, leading 1.02, tracking −0.012em.
- *Edge print* (labels, frame tags, prices, nav numbers): Anybody 600–800, width 72–78 %, upper case,
  tracking +0.07em, 12–13 px — the text a film maker exposes along the edge of the strip.
- *Bench notes* (everything you read): Hubot Sans 400, 16–17 px, leading 1.62, measure 60–68ch.

Scale — ratio 1.25 (major third) from a 1rem base, fluid with `clamp()`:

| step | role | size |
|---|---|---|
| −2 | edge print | 0.75rem |
| −1 | small / captions | 0.875rem |
| 0 | body | clamp(1rem, .97rem + .16vw, 1.0625rem) |
| 1 | lead | clamp(1.125rem, 1.06rem + .34vw, 1.3125rem) |
| 2 | h3 | clamp(1.25rem, 1.16rem + .45vw, 1.5625rem) |
| 3 | h2 | clamp(1.6rem, 1.3rem + 1.45vw, 2.4375rem) |
| 4 | h1 | clamp(2.05rem, 1.35rem + 3.3vw, 3.8rem) |
| 5 | numerals / read-outs | clamp(2.6rem, 2rem + 3vw, 4.75rem) |

No layout shift: `display=swap` plus two local fallback faces with `size-adjust` measured against the real
fonts (Anybody 800 = 126 % of Arial Bold; Hubot Sans 400 = 100.3 % of Arial) and matching ascent/descent.

## Spacing
4 px base, ×1.5 after 16: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 36 · 56 · 84 · 128. Section padding is fluid
(`clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)`); frames in a strip sit 10 px apart, the way frames sit on film.

## Grid
Desktop ≥ 960 px: a fixed 13rem film-edge rail on the left, then the light table. Inside it a 12-column
grid, 1.5rem gutters, content capped at 68rem and centred; running text capped at 66ch.
Mobile: one column, 18 px side padding, a 4rem dock fixed to the bottom edge. No top bar on any width.

## Icon family — "bench icons"
32 × 32 grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, 2 px radius on every rectangle, no fills — except one
2.4 px accent dot per icon, the grease dot, on the part that matters (the bevel, the fold, the corner of the
board). Drawn for this trade only: a print with a curled corner, a moulding with its mount, a stretcher bar
corner, a window mount, a book lying flat, a book with a pool ladder, a file with an equals sign, a board
sandwich, a parcel on an outline of the country, a loupe on a ruler, plus the four dock icons.

## Signature device — the strip and the grease pencil
- **Strip**: ink film base, sprocket perforations above and below (drawn as a CSS mask, so the "holes" take the
  colour of whatever the strip lies on), frames 3:2 like 35 mm, a frame number and edge print under each
  frame, the price in the edge print.
- **Grease pencil** (accent, waxy edge from an SVG turbulence filter): a ring = the chosen frame; a cross =
  work we do not take; a tick = sharp enough at this distance.
- It repeats with variation down the home page: (1) a two-strip contact sheet of the six prices; (2) a clear
  "leader" strip of four fact frames; (3) glassine sleeves holding the six bench icons; (4) the light-box
  bench with a live size read-out and paper sizes drawn to scale; (5) a vertical strip of five numbered
  process frames beside an enlargement with crop marks; (6) four crossed-out frames; (7) questions numbered
  like frames; (8) a rigid board mailer as the contact block.
- The rail itself is the device: a vertical film edge with perforations and the name set as edge print.

## Header (assigned, unique in the batch)
Desktop: left side rail — name set vertically as edge print, tagline beside it, six numbered links stacked
below, **Order prints** at the foot. Mobile: no top bar; a short non-sticky film leader carries the name,
and a bottom dock carries Prints · Frames · Sizes · Order as labelled icons plus the word "Index", which
opens the rest. Never three bars.

## Motion
One animation: the ring draws itself (0.9 s, cubic-bezier(.65,.02,.3,1), 150 ms delay) once, when 45 % of
its strip is in view. Hover: a frame's edge print brightens (120 ms). The Index sheet rises 200 ms.
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → rings are simply there, nothing moves, scroll is instant.
Nothing is hidden when JavaScript is off: rings drawn, answers open, banner absent.

## Photography
Stock pictures used as context only — prints on a table, frames on a wall, a blank stretched canvas, a book
lying open. Never presented as this business's own photographs; alt text says what is in the picture.
Small frames 480 px (3:2), enlargements 800 px, every file WebP under 45 KB.
