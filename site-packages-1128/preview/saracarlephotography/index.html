How big can your photo print?

A print is only as sharp as the pixels behind it. Type your photo’s size in pixels, say where the print will be looked at, and the sum is done for you.

inches = pixels ÷ ppi

300 ppi for a print in the hand, 200 on a shelf, 150 across a room; centimetres are inches × 2.54. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Roll 04How we work

How an order runs, from form to parcel.

Sara Carle Photography is a small print room, not a studio. Nobody here takes pictures. We print yours, on our own machines, and five things happen between your order and your doormat.

  1. 1

    You send the order form

    Pick what you want made and tell us where it is going. Sending the form costs nothing and pays for nothing; it simply puts your order on the bench.

  2. 2

    We send an upload link

    Within one working day you get a reply from a person, with a private link for your photographs. Send the originals, straight from the phone or camera — not screenshots, and not copies that have been through a chat app.

  3. 3

    We check every file

    Pixel size against print size, the colour profile, and how the picture sits on the paper shape. If a photo will print soft at the size you asked for, or the crop trims something that matters, you hear about it before any ink goes down.

  4. 4

    You pay by secure link

    Once the files pass, we e-mail the total and a secure payment link. Nothing is charged before that point and nothing is added after it.

  5. 5

    We print, pack and post

    Prints are made giclée in pigment ink and left overnight to settle before they are sleeved. Then it is Original Delivery: flat between rigid boards, tracked, with the tracking number sent to you the day the parcel leaves.

What we care about

We print what is in the file. We do not smooth skin, swap skies or sharpen what the camera did not catch. If a file is good the print will be good, and if it is not, we would rather tell you than take the money and post you a disappointment.

Everything is made to order here in Bradford and posted within the UK. There is no shop counter to visit and no stock on a shelf: your print does not exist until you ask for it.

Roll 05Before you order

Questions worth asking first.

Straight answers about files, sizes, packing and what happens when the post lets a parcel down.

The original, straight off the phone or camera: a JPEG, HEIC, PNG or TIFF at full size. Not a screenshot, and not a copy that has been through a chat app or a social site — those are squeezed down to a fraction of their pixels on the way.

Leave the colour profile as it is. If the file carries none we treat it as sRGB, which is what nearly every phone and camera writes.

Divide the pixels by the ppi. A twelve-megapixel phone photo is 4032 × 3024 pixels. At 300 ppi, the standard for a print held in the hand, that is about 13 × 10 in, so an 8 × 10 in print or an A4 frame is comfortably sharp. On a wall, seen across a room at 150 ppi, the same file stretches to roughly 27 × 20 in.

The print size calculator does the sum for your exact file and marks which of our sizes it fills.
