Stop 2 · After you send it

What happens next

Four steps, no surprises

  1. An automatic note

    The moment the form lands, a short reply tells you it arrived.

  2. A confirmation from a person

    Next working day, at the latest, a person sends the exact total and a card link, plus a note if anything on the order looks off.

  3. You pay through the link

    Nothing is charged until you do. If you change your mind, ignore it and the order lapses.

  4. We pack and dispatch

    Two working days after you pay, at the latest, the carton is out of Grand Rapids and its tracking code is in your inbox.
