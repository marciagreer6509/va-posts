Stop 1 · Terms

Terms of use, kept short

Last updated: November 28, 2026

These terms cover emailmickey.com, the website of Mickey's Mailboxes in Grand Rapids, Michigan. They are short because the site does one thing: it shows six products, helps you size a parcel box, and takes a request to buy.

What the site is for

The pages describe our mailboxes, parcel boxes, posts and number plates, with their sizes and their prices in US dollars. The parcel box sizer is a calculator. The order form sends us a request. None of it is advice about your property, and no page stands in for a tape measure held against your own wall.

Using it fairly

Use the site to read, to measure and to order. Do not send the form with someone else’s details, do not push automated traffic through it, and do not try to get at parts of the server that are not pages. We may block an address that does.

A form is a request, not a contract

Sending the order form does not buy anything. A sale exists only when we have e-mailed you a confirmation with a total and you have paid through the link in it. Until then either of us can walk away. We may turn a request down, for an address off our route or a product we cannot supply that week, and if we do we tell you.

You pay the price these pages showed when your form went in. If a price or a measurement on a page turns out to be wrong, we say so before any payment is taken and you decide whether to go ahead.

The sizer

The sizer works from the measurements we publish and the numbers you type. It is a sound guide and its sums are shown so you can check them, but cartons vary; measure your own before you rely on its pick.

Who owns what

The words, drawings, icons and the sizer on this site belong to Mickey's Mailboxes. Quote a sentence with a link back and we will be pleased; copying pages wholesale is not allowed. Each photograph stays the property of whoever took it; we use it on the license shown in the credits list further down.

Limits on what we answer for

We take care that this site is accurate and available, but we do not promise it is free of mistakes or interruptions. As far as Michigan law allows, our responsibility for loss arising from the use of this website is limited to the amount you paid for the order concerned. None of this trims the protections a shopper already has by law.

Which law applies

The law of the State of Michigan governs these terms and every box ordered here. Any dispute that cannot be settled by an e-mail between us belongs in the state or federal courts sitting in Kent County, Michigan.

If these terms change

We may revise this page. When we do, the date at the top moves, and each order follows the wording that was live when its form came in.

Reaching us

Questions about any of this go to boxes@emailmickey.com. Shipping is covered on the shipping and dispatch page and sending things back on the returns and exchanges page.
