Ad text

For nine years, Denise and I split a plate of lemon bars at book club. Every month. “I will if you will.”

Last month my hand stopped over the plate.

Not because I was being good. Because for the first time I can remember… I didn’t want one.

Denise’s text that night: “you left me.”
